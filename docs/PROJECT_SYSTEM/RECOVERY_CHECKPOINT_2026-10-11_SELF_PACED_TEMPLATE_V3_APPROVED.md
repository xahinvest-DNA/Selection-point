## 3. Binding methodological boundaries

- fact ≠ interpretation;
- unknown is valid;
- late noticing is valid;
- direction ≠ route ≠ next step ≠ realized action;
- understanding ≠ selection ≠ realization;
- reality feedback outranks explanatory elegance;
- one operation per lesson;
- Stage 1 does not perform repeating-scenario analysis or global underlying-structure diagnosis;
- no single weakness becomes the default center of attention;
- safety has priority over exercise completion;
- questionnaire telemetry is separate from lesson work and questionnaire redesign is not authorized.

## 4. Source synthesis

The approved lesson architecture treats Williams, Millman and Fritz as design inputs, not as a single theory:

- Fritz: created direction + factual current reality;
- Millman: ordinary life + present action;
- Williams: level-appropriate maps/tools and developmental discipline;
- Selection Point: episode-level mechanism, realization/feedback separation and Stage 1/Stage 2 boundary.
