## 2. Approved three-part Orientation entry

Orientation supplies:

```text
1. Чего я хочу?
   → participant-owned desired result / direction

2. Что есть сейчас?
   → factual current position relative to that result

3. Что я реально делаю или не делаю сейчас в этой области?
   → initial general description of current realized movement
```

Output 3 is part of factual current reality but remains distinguishable from the current position itself.

It is **not** a causal conclusion or verified frequency statement. Later concrete cases may confirm, qualify or contradict the learner's initial description.

## 3. Approved Lesson 1 learner journey v4

Controlling decision:

`docs/COURSE/DECISION_STAGE_1_LESSON_1_LEARNER_JOURNEY_V4_APPROVED_2026-10-11.md`.

Lesson 1 teaches one operation:

> **Take one general statement about what I actually do or do not do now and ground it in one concrete case where this really happened.**

Approved seven-step path:

```text
1. return to the three Orientation outputs
2. choose one general action / inaction statement
3. see one complete worked example
4. remember one recent, sufficiently well-remembered concrete case
5. record it in a small case card
6. check whether the case is usable without overclaiming
7. see the result and carry the same observation into ordinary life
```

## 4. Binding research-informed refinements

Three refinements are now part of the approved design:

1. Orientation output 3 remains an initial general self-description rather than verified frequency or causality.
2. The first independent case should preferably be recent and sufficiently well remembered to reduce avoidable retrospective reconstruction.
3. Field observation captures the **next suitable situation**, not only another event that confirms the learner's initial belief. Reality must be allowed to confirm, qualify or contradict the initial description.

## 5. Concrete-case memory boundary

A chosen case must be identifiable enough to work with. At minimum the learner must be able to recover:

- approximately when / in what situation it happened;
- what happened;
- what they actually did or did not do;
- the immediate observable result.

For these core identifying elements, `не знаю / не помню` is not a normal completed answer. If the learner cannot recover enough of the event, they select another case or wait for a naturally occurring future case.

`Не знаю / не наблюдал` remains valid for genuine unknowns such as causes, unobserved details and whether one case represents a broader pattern.

## 6. Approved case card

Required:

1. **Когда / в какой ситуации это было?**
2. **Что тогда происходило?**
3. **Что вы реально сделали или не сделали?**
4. **Что произошло сразу после?**

Optional:

5. **Был ли до этого конкретный план или намерение? Если да — какое?**

The optional intention field is used only when such an intention genuinely existed.

## 7. Simple learner-language rule

Internal methodological precision and learner-facing language remain separate layers.

The learner should not have to learn Selection Point terminology in order to perform the Selection Point operation.

Prefer ordinary language such as:

- «Чего я хочу?»
- «Что есть сейчас?»
- «Что я реально делаю сейчас?»
- «Возьмите один пункт».
- «Вспомните один недавний конкретный случай, который вы хорошо помните».
- «Что тогда происходило?»
- «Что вы реально сделали?»

Internal terms such as `episode`, `релевантный эпизод`, `realization`, `reality check` and similar vocabulary remain internal unless genuinely needed for the learner's next action.
