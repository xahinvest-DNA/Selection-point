## 2. Binding practice boundaries

Gate F must preserve the following distinctions:

1. one narrow lane rather than whole-person scanning;
2. rehearsal ≠ real-world execution evidence;
3. automaticity itself is neutral;
4. interruption is selective and requires meaningful mismatch;
5. unsafe/time-critical skilled activity must not be disrupted by introspective interruption;
6. prepared alternatives remain revisable after new facts;
7. environment-produced improvement ≠ independent participant re-entry;
8. questionnaire/review is both telemetry and a possible intervention/scaffold;
9. support provenance must remain visible;
10. A/B/C support provenance is not a mastery ranking;
11. selected ≠ realized ≠ consequence ≠ used feedback;
12. lapse ≠ total reset;
13. lapse updates should be actionable/testable rather than hidden-cause stories;
14. useful automatic performance should remain automatic while aligned;
15. immediate next-position update and delayed consequence may be distinct;
16. one or several successful cycles do not prove re-automatization;
17. external/professional support may be a valid realized continuation;
18. no fixed repetition, duration or mastery threshold is approved.
