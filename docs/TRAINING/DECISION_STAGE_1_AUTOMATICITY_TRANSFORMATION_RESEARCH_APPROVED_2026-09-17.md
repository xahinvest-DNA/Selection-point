## Decision

The Owner approves the Stage 1 automaticity-transformation research package and its Red Team constraints.

Approved working architecture:

```text
useful automatic default
+
selective interruptibility / rapid re-entry when meaningful mismatch appears
+
training of a new continuation until it becomes easier, faster and more automatic
```

The project therefore does not treat conscious control as the endpoint. The long-term training direction is efficient automaticity that remains interruptible when current reality, consequences, safety, task or direction make re-entry relevant.

## Binding boundaries

- intention is not realization;
- automatic continuation is broader than habit;
- interruption of an old route is not equivalent to formation of a new automatic route;
- old learning may remain retrievable after new learning;
- scaffold/reminder effects must remain visible;
- replacement behavior must stay reality-coupled;
- high-load robust transfer is not required for Stage 1;
- return of an old response does not by itself prove failure or weak intention;
- the training target is not maximum conscious monitoring but selective interruptibility and increasingly efficient new responding.
