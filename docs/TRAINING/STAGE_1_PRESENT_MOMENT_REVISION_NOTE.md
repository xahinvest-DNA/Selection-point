# Stage 1 Present-Moment Revision Note

**ID:** SP-TR-S1-PMR-001  
**Status:** methodological_revision_candidate  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Scope:** Stage 1 training layer only; does not amend Foundation canon by itself.

## 1. Trigger

During Gate C review the Owner challenged an assumption that had remained inside the training architecture:

> To regain participation, does the learner actually need to reconstruct why the automatic episode happened?

The concern is that causal reconstruction can move attention away from the only place where Selection Point can operate: the current position and the continuation that has not yet happened.

## 2. Owner-provided illustration

**ILLUSTRATION — not empirical proof.**

The Owner described a past group of people who became highly skilled at identifying hidden needs, psychological mechanisms, childhood wounds and explanatory origins of problems. Their analyses could become deep and coherent. The relationship ended around a simple unresolved question:

> What do we do with this now?

Years later, according to the Owner's direct report, the same people were still engaged in similar analysis and their lives had not materially changed in the relevant sense.

This case does **not** prove that causal reflection is useless or that the people had no changes in other domains. It is retained because it exposes a failure mode directly relevant to Selection Point:

```text
increasing explanatory sophistication
≠
increasing participation in the next continuation
```

## 3. Core methodological shift

Previous training logic could be read as:

```text
what happened
→ why it happened
→ where participation was available
→ what to change
```

Revision candidate:

```text
what is happening now
→ what has already happened
→ what is still unfolding / has not happened yet
→ where participation is available now
→ what continuation is selected
→ what is actually realized
→ what happens next
```

The causal question is removed from the mandatory path.

## 4. Present-moment rule

> **The learner does not need to know why an automatic process arose in order to notice that it is occurring and alter a continuation that has not yet happened.**

This does not ban history, memory or reflection. It changes their role.

Past material is relevant only insofar as it exists in the current position as data, for example:

- an action already performed;
- consequences already created;
- a promise or plan that still constrains the current position;
- a remembered fact needed for the next move;
- a current bodily condition created by earlier behavior;
- a repeated history that is currently known as history.

The learner is not required to infer a hidden historical cause.

## 5. Strong distinction

```text
notice what is present now
≠
explain how it was caused
```

```text
use past facts that matter now
≠
search for the origin of the person or problem
```

```text
recognize an already-realized consequence
≠
prove why the original action happened
```

## 6. New candidate Stage 1 center

The practical center becomes:

> **Notice the automatic process as something occurring now, distinguish what has already happened from what is still open, and participate in the next continuation that remains available.**

Key questions become:

```text
What is happening now?
What here is an external fact, internal event, interpretation, impulse or already-realized action/consequence?
What has already happened and cannot be undone?
What has not happened yet?
Where can I still participate now?
What continuation do I choose?
What did I actually realize?
What happened next?
```

The question `Why did this happen?` is not required.

## 7. Consequence for retrospective work

Retrospective entry remains valid but changes function.

If the learner notices only after the main event:

```text
past action already happened
→ its consequences exist now
→ current thoughts / impulses / repair options exist now
→ the next continuation has not yet happened
```

The learner does not need to locate a hypothetical lost choice point in the past. The relevant Selection Point is sought in the current position.

This reduces hindsight bias and self-blame.

## 8. Consequence for mechanism language

Candidate contributor families such as appraisal, self-image, local priority or habit may remain useful for designer research, but they should not be mandatory learner-facing explanations.

A Stage 1 success must be possible while the causal explanation remains:

```text
unknown
```

If an explanatory hypothesis does not change present discrimination, available action or later reality testing, it is not required by Stage 1.

## 9. Consequence for the Stage 1 anti-trap

The existing anti-trap remains valid but can be sharpened:

> **Я понял, как это происходит, — и снова заменил выбор пониманием.**

Present-moment extension:

> **Я объясняю, почему это произошло, вместо того чтобы заметить, что происходит сейчас и что ещё могу сделать.**

This extension is a training-layer candidate, not Foundation canon.

## 10. Required downstream revisions

Before Gate C can be approved:

1. propose a present-centered revision of the Stage 1 Capability Spec;
2. remove causal/function reconstruction from mandatory trainable operations;
3. make historical reconstruction optional and instrumental rather than central;
4. rebuild the Psychological Mechanism Map around current-process observability and participation;
5. re-run Red Team against the risk of shallow presentism, denial of history, impulsive action and loss of trajectory context;
6. keep RC-018 binding: selected ≠ realized ≠ consequence ≠ used feedback.

## 11. What this revision does not mean

It does not mean:

- the past is irrelevant;
- causes never matter;
- trauma, history, learning or habit have no effects;
- planning is unnecessary;
- reflection is forbidden;
- every current impulse should be acted upon;
- current experience is infallible;
- professional assessment can be replaced by present-moment noticing.

The narrower claim is:

> **Causal explanation is not a prerequisite for restoring participation in the next available continuation.**
