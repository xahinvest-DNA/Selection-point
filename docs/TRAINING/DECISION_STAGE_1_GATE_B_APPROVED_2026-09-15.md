## What is approved

The Research Packet is approved as the evidence-and-synthesis basis for the next training-development gate. This does **not** convert every synthesis or candidate grouping inside the packet into canon or into an established psychological mechanism.

In particular, the previous M1–M5 working grouping is not approved as five mechanisms.

## Mandatory Gate C constraints

Gate C must begin from classification, not from a predetermined mechanism count.

Every candidate item must distinguish at least:

```text
phenomenon
candidate mechanism / contributing process
trainable operation
epistemic status
observability
competing explanation
falsifier / weakening condition
stage boundary
```

Additional binding constraints:

1. `fusion` / automatic assembly may describe experienced closure without being asserted as a single hidden causal mechanism;
2. `operational task` remains a testable functional hypothesis and must permit `unknown`, competing functions and non-psychological explanations;
3. participation recovery is primarily a trainable capability/operation unless independent grounds justify mechanism status;
4. RC-018 remains a cross-cutting verification boundary, not automatically a psychological mechanism;
5. P12/P13 primarily constrain learning quality, transition and false mastery rather than automatically describing causes of the original episode;
6. P09/body remains a cross-cutting channel/context unless a narrower causal claim is separately justified;
7. Stage 1 trajectory use must stay local enough not to require Stage 2 scenario detection or Stage 4 multi-timeframe trajectory skill;
8. AI/system support may preserve structure but may not manufacture learner content and then count agreement as capability;
9. historical psychological availability of an alternative move remains uncertain when it was not observed;
10. no mechanism may become self-sealing: the map must preserve competing explanations and conditions under which a proposed relation weakens.
