## 1. Purpose

Gate H decides the status of Stage 1 as a whole.

It does not add new curriculum by default.

It must answer:

> **Given the approved architecture and the available evidence, what exactly is Stage 1 justified to claim now, what remains untested, and what is the next legitimate move?**

## 2. Review inputs

Gate H must review together:

- Stage 1 Capability Spec;
- Gate B research;
- Gate C Psychological Mechanism Map;
- Automaticity Neutrality boundary;
- Automaticity Transformation Research;
- Measurement Reactivity observation;
- Gate D Learning Units;
- Gate E Practice Protocol;
- Gate F Observability / Measurement;
- Gate G Pilot Evidence Packet;
- all Red Team constraints and Owner decisions.

## 3. Required review questions

### H1 — Capability coherence
Does the final Stage 1 architecture still serve the approved capability:

> notice enough of the relevant current process, distinguish what is already happened from what remains open, and participate in the next continuation?

### H2 — Practice coherence
Does the Practice Protocol train the capability without:
- permanent self-monitoring;
- anti-automaticity bias;
- willpower heroics;
- hidden-cause archaeology;
- unsafe interruption?

### H3 — Measurement coherence
Can the current measurement architecture distinguish:
- intention from realization;
- prompt-supported from participant-initiated performance;
- environment-supported from participant re-entry;
- aligned automatic performance from conscious SP cycle;
- consequence from used feedback?

### H4 — Evidence sufficiency
What claims are supported by the current corpus, and what claims remain explicitly unsupported?

### H5 — Contradiction handling
Did Gate G reveal any contradiction serious enough to reopen Gate D, E or F?

### H6 — Stage closure status
Choose one of:

```text
A — CLOSE_STAGE_1_ARCHITECTURE
architecture coherent; empirical validation remains limited

B — REQUIRE_POST_PROTOCOL_PILOT_BEFORE_STAGE_1_CLOSURE
architecture coherent but Stage 1 closure requires direct test of final protocol

C — RETURN_TO_GATE_E_OR_F
evidence reveals correctable protocol/measurement flaws

D — RETURN_TO_EARLIER_GATE
capability/mechanism architecture contradicted

E — HOLD
insufficient basis for a legitimate decision
```

Gate H must justify the selected status.

## 4. Important distinction: architecture closure vs efficacy validation

Gate H must not collapse:

```text
Stage 1 architecture is coherent and approved
≠
Stage 1 training efficacy is empirically demonstrated
```

It is legitimate for Stage 1 architecture to close while efficacy remains unvalidated, **if** the project governance treats later pilot work as validation rather than unfinished architecture.

Conversely, if Stage 1 is defined as incomplete until direct post-protocol evidence exists, Gate H must choose status B.

This is an explicit governance decision, not something Gate G can decide implicitly.

## 5. Required evidence table

The Stage Review should classify claims into:

- APPROVED architecture;
- SUPPORTED by current evidence;
- HYPOTHESIS;
- OPEN;
- CONTRADICTED / rejected;
- NOT YET TESTED.

Examples requiring explicit classification:

- intention ≠ realization;
- urge ≠ action;
- one lane ≠ whole person;
- NOTICE ≠ OPEN;
- questionnaire may be intervention;
- planning may improve accessibility;
- lower-load retrieval may emerge;
- re-automatization;
- reopenability;
- transfer;
- prompt dependence / internalization;
- late-entry recovery;
- environment-first support.

## 6. Required governance outputs

Gate H must define:

1. final Stage 1 status;
2. claims Stage 1 is allowed to make;
3. claims Stage 1 is forbidden to make;
4. unresolved empirical questions;
5. required follow-up evidence;
6. whether a post-protocol pilot is next;
7. whether external users may be involved;
8. whether Stage 2 may open;
9. whether any earlier Gate must reopen;
10. what is promoted to SSOT.

## 7. Red Team requirement

Before Owner review, Stage Review Red Team must attack:

- premature closure;
- moving goalposts;
- confusing architecture approval with efficacy;
- using weak evidence to justify Stage 2;
- keeping Stage 1 artificially open forever;
- requiring impossible proof;
- ignoring measurement reactivity;
- overvaluing Owner self-pilot;
- underweighting contradictory evidence;
- smuggling new curriculum into review;
- ambiguous transition to next stage.

## 8. Non-goals

Gate H must not:
- invent a new Stage 1 exercise;
- create a new score;
- declare clinical efficacy;
- open Stage 2 automatically;
- authorize external-user pilot without explicit governance decision;
- modify Foundation canon automatically.

## 9. Completion criterion

Gate H is ready for Owner review when it presents one explicit stage-status recommendation, a claim-boundary table, unresolved evidence questions, and a concrete next authorized move.

Stage 2 remains unopened until explicit Owner decision.
