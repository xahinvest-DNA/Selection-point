# 1. Review question

Do the four proposed Learning Units provide the smallest learner-facing decomposition of Stage 1 while preserving present-centered participation, automaticity neutrality, selective interruption, actual realization, reality feedback, movement toward useful automaticity, and visibility of external scaffolding / questionnaire reactivity — without importing later-stage mastery or converting SP into continuous self-control?

# 2. Verdict

**PASS WITH REQUIRED REFINEMENTS.**

The four-unit compression is strong:

~~~text
NOTICE
→ OPEN
→ REALIZE
→ LEARN
~~~

It is materially simpler than the Gate C designer map and retains the practical center of Stage 1.

However, several distinctions must be tightened before Owner approval.

# 3. Main attacks

## RT-LU-01 — LU-4 risks turning long-term re-automatization into a Stage 1 completion criterion

The approved Stage 1 capability requires a relevant current process to become distinguishable, an open participation point to be found, a continuation to be realized and consequence to become available.

It does not yet require proof that a replacement response has become automatic.

**Required refinement:**

~~~text
Stage 1 cycle completion:
notice → open → realize → consequence/update

longer learning direction:
repeat across recurrences → easier retrieval → possible useful automaticity
~~~

LU-4 may introduce repetition as the direction of training, but no recurrence count or re-automaticity evidence is required for a single Stage 1 cycle.

## RT-LU-02 — noticing and interruption are related but not identical

Noticing may itself interrupt some automatic continuations, but this cannot be assumed.

A person can clearly notice:

> “I am doing it again.”

while continuing unchanged.

**Required refinement:**

~~~text
noticing ≠ interruption
~~~

LU-2 remains justified.

## RT-LU-03 — OPEN can become an unnecessary control ritual

If every noticed impulse triggers a deliberate pause, the learner may interfere with efficient skilled automaticity and create hesitation.

**Required refinement:** LU-2 is conditional on relevant mismatch. No mismatch requiring participation means no interruption duty. The learner is not rewarded for interrupting more often.

## RT-LU-04 — detecting relevant mismatch can smuggle later-stage direction competence into Stage 1

A learner may not know whether a whole life direction is valid.

**Required refinement:** Stage 1 needs only a local operational anchor: current task, safety, explicit commitment, immediate responsibility, already-declared local direction, or observed consequence. No final life-purpose adjudication is required.

## RT-LU-05 — a prepared if–then response can execute automatically without demonstrated Stage 1 noticing

Suppose:

~~~text
cue → trained alternative response
~~~

occurs automatically.

That may be an excellent long-term outcome. But if the learner did not notice/re-enter during the episode, the event alone does not demonstrate the approved Stage 1 noticing capability.

**Required refinement:**

~~~text
effective automated replacement
≠
evidence of Stage 1 Selection Point operation
~~~

SP may eventually train beyond the need for conscious re-entry, but later observability must not use successful automated behavior alone as proof of present Stage 1 operation.

## RT-LU-06 — questionnaire-supported noticing may be measurement reactivity, memory enhancement or social demand rather than internalized capacity

The current Owner observation is valuable but underdetermined.

Possible pathways include prospective monitoring, improved encoding/memory, demand characteristics, self-presentation, discrepancy monitoring, genuine in-the-moment participation, or combinations.

**Required refinement:** Learning Units may use the questionnaire as a scaffold hypothesis but must not narrate a guaranteed progression from prompt to internal capacity.

Prefer:

~~~text
prompted performance
→ observe whether participant-initiated noticing later emerges
~~~

The causal pathway remains OPEN.

## RT-LU-07 — scaffold fading belongs to later protocol/measurement design, not Gate D

Gate D can specify that support-dependence matters. It should not yet specify withdrawal schedules, prompt reduction or experimental cadence.

**Required refinement:** keep scaffold dependence vs internalization as a required later observation, not a Gate D procedure.

## RT-LU-08 — LU-3 combines selection and realization, but the merge is acceptable only if RC-018 remains explicit

Learner simplicity favors one unit. But the data architecture must still preserve:

~~~text
selected ≠ realized
~~~

**Required refinement:** retain the merge learner-facing; never merge the records/observability fields.

## RT-LU-09 — late detection needs a first-class path, not a footnote

If the main action already occurred, the learner can still stop escalation, reduce consequence, repair, seek help, or alter the next-cycle condition.

**Required refinement:** every learner-facing unit sequence must support a late-entry variant:

~~~text
main action already happened
→ notice consequence/current continuation
→ find remaining open edge
→ realize repair / reduction / next-cycle preparation
~~~

This protects Stage 1 from becoming “catch yourself before you fail.”

## RT-LU-10 — LEARN can be confused with outcome evaluation

A desirable outcome does not prove capability or universal correctness. An undesirable outcome does not prove the Selection Point failed.

**Required refinement:** LU-4 must ask only:

~~~text
what happened?
what does this change in the current position?
what remains supported / unsupported?
~~~

It must not automatically convert consequence into causal certainty.

## RT-LU-11 — replacement can become rigid rule-following

Yesterday's useful replacement can become tomorrow's outdated automaticity.

**Required refinement:** the mature endpoint must always preserve:

> **automatic default + selective interruptibility under meaningful mismatch.**

Re-automatization without mismatch sensitivity is not the target.

## RT-LU-12 — some automatic continuations exceed self-training scope

In addiction, acute crisis, severe psychiatric symptoms, medical impairment or unsafe environments, a small self-directed interruption may be insufficient or inappropriate.

**Required refinement:** Stage 1 Learning Units must permit:

~~~text
available participation = seek / use professional or external support
~~~

and must not frame failure to self-interrupt as a capability or moral defect.

# 4. Recommended revised architecture

Retain four units, but narrow LU-4:

~~~text
LU-1 NOTICE
notice enough of the relevant current process

LU-2 OPEN
make the relevant old continuation non-exclusive

LU-3 REALIZE
choose relative to reality/local anchor and actually execute

LU-4 UPDATE
observe consequence and update the current position
~~~

Then place re-automatization as a cross-cycle learning direction, not a single-cycle completion requirement:

~~~text
repeated SP cycles
→ useful response retrieval may become easier
→ useful response may become increasingly automatic
→ mismatch sensitivity must remain available
~~~

This is cleaner than naming LU-4 LEARN / REPEAT.

# 5. Measurement/scaffold interpretation after attack

Preserve three evidence levels:

~~~text
LEVEL A — externally prompted
reminder/questionnaire directly triggered noticing or action

LEVEL B — anticipatory scaffold-linked
no immediate prompt, but participant noticed partly because future reporting/support remained salient

LEVEL C — participant-initiated
relevant noticing/re-entry occurred without identifiable current scaffold activation
~~~

These are provenance categories, not mastery levels. They must not yet be scored or thresholded.

# 6. Learner-facing compression recommendation

Candidate compact sequence:

> **Заметь → открой → сделай → сверься с реальностью.**

Longer training direction:

> **Повтори полезное достаточно раз, чтобы думать об этом приходилось всё меньше — но сохрани возможность снова заметить, когда реальность изменилась.**

The second sentence is a design principle, not a Stage 1 completion criterion.

# 7. Gate D recommendation

**REVISE, THEN OWNER REVIEW.**

Required before Owner review:

1. rename/narrow LU-4 from LEARN / REPEAT to consequence/update;
2. move re-automatization to cross-cycle training direction;
3. make late-entry path explicit;
4. preserve local, not ultimate, direction anchor;
5. distinguish automated replacement from evidence of Selection Point operation;
6. preserve scaffold provenance without assuming internalization;
7. retain clinical/support boundary;
8. keep Gate E closed.

# 8. Post-revision verification

The revised \`SP-TR-S1-LU-001\` incorporates all required refinements:

- LU-4 is narrowed to UPDATE / consequence return;
- re-automatization is cross-cycle direction, not one-cycle completion;
- late-entry path is first-class;
- local direction anchors are preserved without importing final life-direction work;
- automated replacement is separated from evidence of current Stage 1 operation;
- scaffold provenance remains visible without assuming internalization;
- clinical / external-support boundary is retained;
- Gate E remains closed.

**Final Red Team verdict:** **PASS WITH BINDING BOUNDARIES — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW.**
