7. participant capture surface is smaller than research trace;
8. episode count is data volume, not performance;
9. noticing timing is descriptive, not mastery;

11. environment-supported improvement ≠ participant re-entry;
12. aligned automatic performance ≠ conscious SP cycle;
13. no observed mismatch → reopenability = not_observed;
14. rehearsal ≠ live evidence;
15. review-elicited update ≠ spontaneous pre-review update;
16. selected ≠ realized ≠ consequence ≠ used feedback;
17. good outcome ≠ proof of capacity;
18. bad outcome ≠ proof of incapacity;
19. lane-local summaries by default;
20. Gate G readiness is evidence-packet coverage capability, not participant event quota.
