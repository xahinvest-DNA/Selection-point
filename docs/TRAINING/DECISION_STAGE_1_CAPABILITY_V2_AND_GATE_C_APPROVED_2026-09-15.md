# Decision — Stage 1 Capability v2 and Gate C Approved

**ID:** SP-TR-DEC-S1-C-002  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Status:** approved  
**Authority:** Owner explicit approval — «утверждаем»

## 1. Decision

The Owner approved the present-centered Stage 1 training revision and the revised Gate C architecture.

Approved capability:

> **Человек учится замечать релевантный автоматический процесс как происходящее сейчас, различать в текущей позиции уже случившееся и ещё открытое, находить реально доступное участие относительно текущей реальности и значимого направления и фактически осуществлять следующее продолжение.**

Compact formulation:

> **Заметить происходящее сейчас и вернуть участие в следующем продолжении.**

`SP-TR-S1-CAP-002` supersedes `SP-TR-S1-CAP-001` at the training layer.

## 2. Approved Gate C center

Gate C is approved around the operation map:

```text
RELEVANT CURRENT POSITION
→ NOTICE ENOUGH
→ DISTINGUISH WHAT MATTERS
→ FIND THE RELEVANT OPEN EDGE
→ CHOOSE RELATIVE TO REALITY + DIRECTION
→ ACTUALLY REALIZE
→ OBSERVE CONSEQUENCE / UPDATED CURRENT POSITION
```

No hidden psychological cause is required.

The approved Gate C artifact becomes `SP-TR-S1-PMM-002`.

## 3. Cause boundary

Approved distinction:

```text
known / testable causal information
≠ speculative hidden psychological origin
```

Selection Point may use observable or testable causal knowledge where operationally useful, but Stage 1 does not require psychological archaeology, childhood-origin explanation, hidden need discovery or a definitive motive before participation becomes possible.

## 4. Present/history boundary

Approved:

> **The present is the place of participation, not an excuse to forget history.**

Relevant accumulated consequences, commitments, prior realized actions and constraints can belong to the current position when they materially affect the next continuation.

## 5. Automaticity neutrality clarification

The Owner additionally clarified that automaticity itself is neither good nor bad; it is an existing mechanism/system.

Approved training boundary:

```text
automaticity ≠ moral failure
automaticity ≠ pathology by default
automaticity ≠ enemy to eliminate
```

Selection Point does not aim to replace automatic functioning with continuous conscious control.

Preferred formulation:

> **Selection Point не воюет с автопилотом. Он возвращает участие там, где продолжение ещё можно изменить.**

This boundary is documented in `STAGE_1_AUTOMATICITY_NEUTRALITY_BOUNDARY.md`.

## 6. Historical text interpretation

The Owner supplied an earlier text describing an automatic/adaptive system and a choice between continuing familiar actions and learning to manage the system.

The current architecture retains:

- knowing ≠ doing;
- automaticity is not morally bad;
- choice itself is not classified as morally good/bad by default;
- information about a process is insufficient without realized participation.

The current architecture does **not** retain literally:

- a two-agent ontology in which a foreign autopilot “controls” the real person;
- total self-control as the goal;
- “creator of your reality” as a causal claim.

Instead:

> **A person participates in forming the next position inside the actually available action space.**
