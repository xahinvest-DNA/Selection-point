# Decision — Stage 1 Gate G Pilot Evidence Packet Approved

**ID:** SP-TR-DEC-S1-G-001  
**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Status:** approved  
**Authority:** Owner explicit approval — «утверждаем»

## 1. Decision

The Owner approved Stage 1 Gate G — Pilot Evidence Packet.

Approved evidence interpretation:

- the 14-record / three-participant corpus is **pre-protocol / transitional compatibility evidence**;
- it is methodologically useful but efficacy-insufficient;
- it supports key distinctions such as intention ≠ realization, urge ≠ action, lane specificity and measurement reactivity;
- it does not establish Gate E efficacy, stable re-automatization, reopenability, transfer or participant mastery.

The approved artifact is `SP-TR-S1-PEP-001`.

## 2. Binding evidence boundaries

Gate H must preserve:

1. the corpus is not a Gate E efficacy test;
2. event chains are partial reconstructions;
3. self-report remains self-report;
4. Owner/designer expectancy is a major confound;
5. P-B/P-C provide cross-case recurrence, not independent efficacy replication;
6. submitted reports are not an unbiased sample of all days/opportunities;
7. intention/realization divergence is supported;
8. lane specificity is supported;
9. urge ≠ action is supported;
10. questionnaire reactivity is observed for the Owner but not generalized;
11. NOTICE timing and OPEN are under-observed;
12. automaticity is not demonstrated, not shown absent;
13. reopenability remains not_observed;
14. proposed OPEN discriminator is itself a reactive measurement experiment;
15. public artifacts remain de-identified;
16. no composite score, efficacy claim, participant ranking or mastery claim is authorized.

## 3. Gate consequences

- Gate G is closed as approved.
- Gate H — Stage 1 Stage Review — is opened.
- Stage 2 is **not** opened automatically.
- Gate H must decide whether Stage 1 is:
  - ready to close as provisionally validated architecture;
  - ready only for a formal post-protocol pilot;
  - in need of revision at Gates D/E/F;
  - or blocked by unresolved contradictions.
- No external-user pilot is authorized by this decision.
- No trainer/software implementation is authorized.
- Foundation Stage 5 remains unopened.
