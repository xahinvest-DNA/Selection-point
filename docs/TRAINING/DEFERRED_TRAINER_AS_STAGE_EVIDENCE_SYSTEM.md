# Deferred Hypothesis — Trainer as Stage Evidence System

**ID:** SP-TR-HYP-001  
**Status:** deferred / parked  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Scope:** preserve the approved discussion for later trainer design without opening trainer implementation now.

## Core hypothesis

The future Selection Point trainer may serve not only as a trajectory visualizer, but as a **stage evidence system**: a system that accumulates traceable, multi-timeframe evidence of how available a stage-specific operation has become in real life.

It must not reduce stage mastery to a compliance score or a single composite metric.

## Two independent trajectories

The trainer should preserve a distinction between:

1. **Reality trajectory** — what the person actually did and what factual positions/consequences followed.
2. **Capability trajectory** — how the person's participation in the formation of the next continuation changed over time.

A better domain outcome does not automatically prove stronger SP capability, and stronger SP evidence does not guarantee the desired external outcome.

## Multi-timeframe structure

Candidate drill-down hierarchy:

```text
EPISODE
→ D1
→ W1
→ M1
→ 3M / 6M / longer review
```

Higher-level aggregation must remain traceable back to lower-level events. A summary line must never erase contradictory episodes or uncertainty.

## Candidate Stage 1 evidence traces

For Stage 1, later trainer design may preserve evidence such as:

- whether a concrete automatic episode became distinguishable;
- when it became distinguishable: after, during, or before the main action;
- whether fact, state, interpretation, activated self-image and operational task were distinguished;
- whether the person identified an actually available move;
- selected continuation vs realized continuation;
- whether any continuation, consequence, recovery action or next-cycle condition was actually changed;
- whether feedback from reality was noticed and used;
- how much external scaffold or prompting was required.

No single item is sufficient by itself to prove stage mastery.

## Scaffold dependence

The system must distinguish at least conceptually between:

```text
self-detected
prompted by general reminder
prompted by structured question
reconstruction largely produced by the system
```

Later validation may require gradually reduced scaffold to test whether the operation remains available without constant trainer prompting.

Prompt exposure is therefore evidence metadata, not a nuisance variable.

## Epistemic levels

The trainer must not visually merge different evidence qualities.

At minimum, preserve distinctions between:

1. **Observed / measured fact** — e.g. weight, training duration, logged action, timestamp.
2. **Self-report** — e.g. "wanted to postpone", "felt anxious", "remembered without prompt".
3. **Interpretation / hypothesis** — e.g. "avoidance mechanism", "operational task was relief".

A coherent interpretation must not be displayed as if it were measured fact.

## Transition logic

Preferred future framing:

> The trainer does not objectively declare that a person has moved to the next stage. It accumulates multi-timeframe, traceable evidence about how repeatedly available the operation of the current stage has become in real life.

Transition review then uses that evidence against the approved qualitative criteria of the stage.

For Stage 1 this may include repeated evidence that the person can:

- localize a concrete episode;
- separate fact from hypothesis;
- reconstruct the basic functional chain;
- identify a realistically available move;
- participate in selecting it;
- actually realize at least some changed continuations;
- observe consequences;
- operate with decreasing dependence on external reconstruction.

## Anti-patterns

The trainer must not:

- equate health compliance with Selection Capacity;
- produce a universal Selection Capacity score without separate validation;
- infer causality from temporal sequence;
- infer mastery from questionnaire completion;
- hide contradictory events behind aggregates;
- treat retrospective explanation as evidence of prior availability;
- confuse prompted realization with unprompted realization;
- promote self-report or researcher interpretation to measured fact.

## Key principle

> **Facts constrain self-description, but facts are only as useful as the observation model that collected and classified them.**

The trainer should protect against both inflated self-assessment and unjustified self-condemnation by showing the actual accumulated trajectory with drill-down to concrete events.

## Project status

This hypothesis is deliberately **parked**.

It does not authorize:
- trainer UI design;
- trainer implementation;
- new metrics;
- numeric stage thresholds;
- a composite score;
- opening SP-LAB-002;
- opening the external-user pilot.

Return to this document only after the current Stage 1 training-material cycle reaches the appropriate observability/measurement design point or after an explicit owner decision.
