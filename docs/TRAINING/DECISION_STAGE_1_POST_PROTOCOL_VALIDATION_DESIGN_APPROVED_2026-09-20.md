## 3. Approved sequencing

```text
Phase 0 — choose lane + Lane Card + safety boundary
Phase 1 — supported acquisition
Phase 2 — reduced-current-prompt observation
Phase 3 — natural continuation / mismatch probe
```

Phase 1 → Phase 2 must not be interpreted as a causal prompt-fading experiment.

## 4. Approved pragmatic coverage

```text
target:
  at least 6 evaluable live recurrences

desirable support-state variation:
  at least 2 supported/current-prompt events where relevant
  at least 2 reduced-current-prompt events where relevant

calendar cap:
  21 days
```

These are research-management rules only.

They are not mastery, efficacy or scientific sufficiency thresholds.

## 5. Approved capture boundary

Live capture remains minimal and should preserve:

- event/marker;
- noticing timing;
- open edge and its timing;
- selected continuation;
- realized continuation;
- active support/provenance;
- immediate consequence;
- later update where relevant;
- future-reporting salience where relevant;
- unknown / not_observed.

Only `in_event` OPEN supports a live OPEN claim.

## 6. Red Team boundaries made binding

- Owner V0 cannot establish independent efficacy.
- Event capture is itself measurement exposure.
- No running success score.
- Safety/medical/protective support is never withdrawn to prove independence.
- Environment-enforced behavior is not participant-initiated OPEN.
- Automaticity and reopenability are opportunistic observations in V0.
- No-event days do not create an invisible opportunity denominator.
- No architecture contradiction observed ≠ architecture confirmed true.
- SP-TR-HYP-002 Directed Question remains parked during V0.
