## 5. Reality authority / reopen rule

Architecture closure is falsifiable.

```text
approved_closed
≠
immune_to_revision
```

An earlier Gate may reopen if direct validation returns a concrete contradiction, including:

- live protocol structurally unusable under realistic load;
- Lane Card consistently creates more burden than access;
- practice induces persistent hypervigilance or unsafe interruption;
- measurement cannot distinguish the claims it is being used to support;
- direct evidence contradicts a core capability distinction;
- important safety or harm signals appear.

Poor outcomes alone do not automatically identify which Gate is wrong.
