# Stage 1 — Automaticity Neutrality Boundary

**ID:** SP-TR-S1-AUTO-NEUTRAL-001  
**Status:** approved training-layer boundary  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Owner clarification:** automaticity is not good or bad; it is an existing mechanism/system.  
**Scope:** Stage 1 training architecture. This document does not amend Foundation canon by itself.

## 1. Central boundary

> **Автоматизм не является врагом, дефектом или моральной ошибкой. Это нормальная часть человеческого функционирования. Selection Point не обучает уничтожать автоматизм; он обучает замечать релевантный автоматический процесс и возвращать участие там, где следующее продолжение ещё открыто.**

The target is therefore not:

```text
automaticity → bad
conscious control → good
```

but:

```text
automatic continuation
+ current reality
+ relevant direction / task / responsibility
→ is participation needed and available now?
```

## 2. What is retained from the Owner's earlier text

The historical text contains three ideas that remain highly compatible with the current Selection Point architecture.

### 2.1 Knowing is not doing

The distinction:

> knowing how to manage / choose is not the same as actually doing it

is preserved in stricter form through RC-018:

```text
understanding ≠ selection
selection ≠ realization
realization ≠ desired result
consequence ≠ used feedback
```

This is directly relevant to the Stage 1 anti-trap:

> **Я понял, как это происходит, — и снова заменил выбор пониманием.**

### 2.2 Automaticity is not morally bad

The earlier text explicitly rejects moral ranking of the available options. That remains useful.

Selection Point does not treat an automatic response as evidence that the person is weak, unconscious, bad, immature or defective.

An automatic continuation may be:

- efficient;
- protective;
- learned from prior successful adaptation;
- resource-saving;
- appropriate to familiar conditions;
- neutral;
- maladaptive only in a changed context;
- mixed: useful locally and costly later.

No single function is assumed in a specific episode without evidence.

### 2.3 The practical question is what happens next

The earlier text correctly separates information from actual management/participation. The current architecture sharpens this further:

```text
not: explain the system completely
but: notice enough of the current position to participate in the next relevant continuation
```

## 3. What is not retained literally

Several formulations in the earlier text are useful historically but too strong for the current project.

### 3.1 “The automatic system manages you”

This can create a false two-agent model:

```text
real me
vs
foreign autopilot
```

Selection Point does not require such an ontology.

More precise:

> **Some continuations can occur with little current participation because learned, state-shaped or context-linked processes make them highly available.**

### 3.2 “Learn to control the system / yourself”

The word `control` can imply continuous executive management of thought, emotion, body and behavior.

Selection Point does not promise or require that.

Preferred distinction:

```text
total control
≠
available participation
```

The goal is not permanent conscious supervision. It is the ability to notice and intervene when current reality, consequences or direction make participation relevant.

### 3.3 “Become the creator of your reality”

This wording overstates authorship and conflicts with existing SP boundaries on external reality, other people, illness, accidents, constraints and uncertainty.

Preferred formulation:

> **Участвовать в формировании следующей позиции внутри реально доступного пространства действий.**

A person influences trajectory through realized continuations but does not create all events or outcomes.

## 4. Adaptive interpretation

At the training layer, `adaptive autopilot` is useful as a metaphor only if it is not personified or moralized.

A conservative interpretation is:

> **Automatic processes are part of the system by which humans act efficiently without reconstructing every decision from zero. Their presence is normal. Their usefulness depends on context, current reality and the consequences they generate.**

This prevents two symmetrical errors:

```text
automatic = bad
and
automatic = always adaptive/right
```

Automaticity can preserve an old solution after the environment, goals, resources or consequences have changed.

Selection Point becomes relevant at that mismatch.

## 5. Stage 1 training implication

The learner should not be trained to ask:

```text
How do I defeat the autopilot?
What is wrong with me?
Which hidden mechanism controls me?
```

The core questions are closer to:

```text
What is happening now that matters?
What has already happened?
What relevant continuation is still open?
What is actually available from this position?
What do I choose relative to current reality and direction?
What did I actually realize?
What happened next?
```

## 6. Non-pathologizing rule

A detected automatic continuation is data, not a failure.

Late noticing is also data, not failure.

If the automatic continuation is currently effective and aligned with reality/direction, there may be no reason to interrupt it.

Therefore:

> **Selection Capacity is not measured by how little automatic behavior a person has.**

A person could be highly capable while using extensive automatic routines and only intervening when a relevant selection point appears.

## 7. Relationship to present-moment revision

This boundary strengthens `SP-TR-S1-CAP-002` and `SP-TR-S1-PMM-002`.

The present-centered architecture is not a war between consciousness and automaticity.

It is:

```text
automatic process may already be unfolding
↓
notice enough of the relevant current position
↓
if participation is relevant and available, recover it
↓
realize the continuation
↓
let consequences update the next position
```

## 8. Evidence boundary

This document does not claim that all automatic processes exist primarily to protect the person, or that every problematic behavior was once adaptive.

Those may be plausible in some cases but are not required Stage 1 explanations.

The approved training boundary is narrower:

```text
automaticity is a normal mechanism
≠ moral failure
≠ pathology by default
≠ enemy to eliminate
```

Its function in a particular episode may remain `unknown`.

## 9. Compact formulas

> **Автоматизм — не ошибка. Это способ работы системы.**

> **Проблема не в том, что часть жизни автоматизирована. Вопрос в том, можешь ли ты заметить момент, когда реальность требует твоего участия.**

> **Selection Point не воюет с автопилотом. Он возвращает участие там, где продолжение ещё можно изменить.**
