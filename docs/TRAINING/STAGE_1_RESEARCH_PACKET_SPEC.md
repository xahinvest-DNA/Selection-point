# Stage 1 Research Packet — Execution Spec

**ID:** SP-TR-S1-RP-SPEC-001  
**Status:** active_in_chat  
**Gate:** B — Stage 1 Research Packet  
**Upstream authority:** `SP-TR-S1-CAP-001`  
**Project operating model:** `SP-OPS-002`  
**Purpose:** define a bounded research task for ordinary Chat that assembles the full approved Stage 1 context without silently creating new Selection Point methodology.

## 1. Task

Produce one research artifact:

`docs/TRAINING/STAGE_1_RESEARCH_PACKET.md`

The packet must reconstruct the complete approved Stage 1 evidence/context needed for the next methodological step, **Gate C — Psychological Mechanism Map**.

This is a research/synthesis task, not a canon-writing task.

## 2. Read order

Read at minimum:

1. `docs/PROJECT_SYSTEM/PROJECT_SYSTEM_STATE.yaml`
2. current recovery checkpoint
3. `docs/PROJECT_SYSTEM/SP_CHAT_OPERATING_MODEL.md`
4. `docs/TRAINING/STAGE_1_CAPABILITY_SPEC.md`
5. `docs/FOUNDATION/PROJECT_STATE.yaml`
6. all approved Stage 1 canonical files under `docs/FOUNDATION/CANONICAL/`
7. all Stage 1 full-theory files under `docs/FOUNDATION/FIVE_STAGES_THEORY/`
8. Stage 1 related entries under `docs/FOUNDATION/LIBRARIES/`
9. Stage 1 related `SOURCE_MATERIALS`, governance notes, Reality Checks and approval addenda
10. cross-cutting material that materially changes interpretation of Stage 1, especially RC-018 choice → realized continuation → feedback
11. relevant Product Lab measurement/evidence boundaries only where they constrain claims about observability or proof

If a source conflicts with a later approved source, preserve the conflict and identify authority/order; do not silently reconcile it.

## 3. Approved Stage 1 capability to protect

The packet must remain anchored to `SP-TR-S1-CAP-001`:

> Человек учится превращать один собственный автоматический эпизод из переживаемой неизбежности в различимый процесс, находить первый реально доступный участок своего участия и фактически менять хотя бы одно дальнейшее продолжение.

Do not broaden this into total self-control, constant awareness, emotional control, guaranteed outcomes, or full Stage 2 pattern recognition.

## 4. Required output structure

### A. Executive research summary
- what Stage 1 is trying to make practically possible;
- what it explicitly does not claim;
- the central research question for Gate C.

### B. Canon inventory
Account for **all approved Stage 1 parameters SP-S1-P01–SP-S1-P13**.

For each parameter record:
- canonical meaning;
- practical function inside Stage 1;
- relation to the Stage 1 capability spec;
- supporting full-theory/source/governance references;
- important boundaries or later clarifications;
- unresolved ambiguity, if any.

No approved parameter may disappear merely because it seems redundant.

### C. Cross-cutting boundaries affecting Stage 1
At minimum inspect:
- fact vs perception/model;
- activated self-image vs whole self;
- actually available participation point;
- explanation vs participation;
- selected continuation vs realized continuation;
- consequence vs used feedback;
- conscious non-action and partial realization where relevant;
- epistemic distinction between observed fact, self-report, inference and interpretation.

### D. Source examples and cases
Collect concrete examples already present in approved project material.

For every example distinguish:
- observed/factual description;
- participant interpretation;
- project interpretation;
- what the example can support;
- what it cannot support.

Do not upgrade illustrative cases into empirical proof.

### E. Contradictions and tension map
List meaningful tensions such as:
- different formulations that may imply different mechanisms;
- old language that became narrower after later clarifications;
- places where a clean explanatory sequence risks implying causality not actually established;
- overlaps with Stage 2 or later stages;
- any conflict between teaching simplicity and methodological precision.

Classify each item as one of:
`resolved_by_later_authority`, `open`, `wording_only`, `possible_methodological_conflict`.

### F. Candidate psychological mechanisms
Propose candidate mechanisms only as **research hypotheses for Gate C**.

For each candidate include:
- working name;
- what process it attempts to explain;
- which Stage 1 parameters it may unify;
- evidence/source basis;
- competing explanation(s);
- what would falsify or weaken the grouping;
- whether it is likely trainable as one unit or may need decomposition.

Do **not** decide the final Psychological Mechanism Map.

### G. Open questions
Separate questions into:
- methodological;
- psychological/empirical;
- instructional;
- observability/measurement;
- boundary-with-other-stage.

State which questions must be resolved before Gate C can be approved and which may remain empirical hypotheses.

### H. Evidence boundaries
State clearly what available material does and does not justify.

At minimum preserve:
- coherent explanation ≠ proven causality;
- questionnaire completion ≠ SP capability;
- insight ≠ realized change;
- selected continuation ≠ realized continuation;
- desired outcome ≠ proof of Stage 1 capability;
- one successful episode ≠ mastery;
- prompted performance ≠ independent capability;
- self-report and project interpretation are not the same epistemic category as externally observable fact.

### I. Gate C handoff
End with a compact handoff containing:
- candidate mechanism clusters;
- parameters covered by each cluster;
- unresolved overlaps;
- questions that require methodological decision;
- explicit statement that the packet is `ready_for_red_team`, not approved methodology.

## 5. Epistemic labels

Use visible labels throughout where useful:

- **APPROVED** — explicit approved project meaning;
- **DERIVED** — conservative synthesis directly implied by approved material;
- **HYPOTHESIS** — candidate explanation/grouping requiring review or evidence;
- **OPEN** — unresolved question;
- **CONTRADICTION** — material tension not safely removable by synthesis;
- **ILLUSTRATION** — example that explains but does not prove;
- **OBSERVED** — directly recorded fact/behavior;
- **SELF-REPORT** — participant account;
- **INTERPRETATION** — project/analyst reading.

Never convert a hypothesis into approved meaning through confident wording.

## 6. Forbidden moves

During Gate B, do not:
- rewrite or amend Foundation canon;
- open Stage 5;
- open `SP-LAB-002`;
- open an external-user pilot;
- define numeric mastery thresholds;
- create a composite Selection Capacity score;
- design trainer implementation;
- turn the 13 Stage 1 parameters mechanically into 13 lessons;
- finalize the Psychological Mechanism Map;
- create a full lesson sequence or practice protocol;
- make medical claims from the body/health pilot;
- treat the owner self-pilot as efficacy proof;
- treat chronology/correlation as causality;
- erase conflicting or inconvenient source material.

## 7. Research quality requirements

The packet is acceptable only if:

1. all `SP-S1-P01–SP-S1-P13` are explicitly accounted for;
2. every substantive claim is traceable to repository source(s) or clearly labeled hypothesis;
3. later approved clarifications outrank earlier provisional wording without erasing historical tension;
4. examples preserve fact / self-report / interpretation distinctions;
5. the capability spec is used as an organizing constraint rather than rewritten;
6. Stage 1 / Stage 2 boundary is examined explicitly;
7. RC-018 is integrated where relevant without retroactively inventing observations;
8. unresolved questions are surfaced rather than smoothed over;
9. the result is useful for deciding the smallest coherent set of trainable mechanisms at Gate C;
10. final status is `ready_for_red_team`.

## 8. Execution rule

Ordinary Chat executes this spec directly. No separate product mode is required.

Chat may search the repository broadly, compare documents, build traceability tables and identify stale or conflicting formulations.

Chat may propose mechanism clusters, but must not approve them without Owner decision.

If evidence is missing, use `unknown`, `not_observed` or `open` rather than filling gaps from intuition.

If the task reveals a genuine unresolved methodological decision, stop that branch of synthesis at the decision boundary and record it for review.

The final artifact must end with:

```text
Methodological approval: pending
Next authority: Chat Red Team → Owner approval
Next possible gate after approval: Gate C — Psychological Mechanism Map
```
