# Decision — Lesson 1 Brief Returned for Architectural Revision

**ID:** SP-COURSE-DEC-S1-L1-BRIEF-REVISION-001  
**Date:** 11 October 2026

**Authority:** Owner explicit decision — «Да, возвращаем на доработку»

## Decision

`docs/COURSE/STAGE_1_LESSON_1_WORK_BRIEF.md` version 2 is **not approved** and is returned for revision.

## Reason

The draft begins too quickly with episode localization. This creates a methodological risk that the learner experiences the practical course as beginning from a problem episode rather than from:

```text
created result / direction
+
factual current reality
```

The next revision must reconsider the boundary between Orientation and Lesson 1 using the Fritz creative-orientation frame.

Episode work may remain, but only if it is positioned as a means of making current reality concrete rather than as the organizing center of the course.

## Required redesign questions

The revised brief must determine:

1. whether created result/direction + factual current reality remain in a separate mandatory Orientation or move into Lesson 1;
2. what the one main operation of Lesson 1 is;
3. how episode localization relates to that operation;
4. how to avoid turning the course into problem-solving around unwanted behavior;
5. what observable output of Lesson 1 becomes the input for Lesson 2;
6. whether the approved five-core-lesson structure can be preserved without a separate structure revision.

## Authorization boundary

Authorized:

- redesign of the Lesson 1 work brief;
- analysis of the Orientation ↔ Lesson 1 boundary required for that redesign.

Not authorized:

- new learner-facing Lesson 1;
- Lesson 2 development;
- Stage 2 or Foundation S5;
- external pilot;
- trainer implementation;
- questionnaire redesign.
