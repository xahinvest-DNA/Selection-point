## 1. Decision

The revised Lesson 1 learner journey is approved under the current three-part Orientation entry.

Orientation supplies:

```text
1. Чего я хочу?
2. Что есть сейчас?
3. Что я реально делаю или не делаю сейчас в этой области?
```

The third output remains an **initial general self-description of current movement**. It is not yet a verified frequency claim, recurring pattern or causal explanation.

Lesson 1 trains one operation:

> **Take one general statement about what I actually do or do not do now and ground it in one concrete case where this really happened.**

Learner-facing language must remain ordinary and simple.

## 2. Approved seven-step learner journey

### Step 1 — Return to the Orientation outputs

Show the learner their three Orientation results without recreating Orientation work.

The learner sees:

- what they want;
- what is true now;
- what they currently describe themselves as actually doing or not doing.

### Step 2 — Choose one general action statement

The learner selects one statement from Orientation output 3 to examine more closely.

Selection may concern useful, neutral or unwanted action / inaction. The lesson must not default to the weakest, worst or most shameful behavior.

### Step 3 — See one complete worked example

Before independent work, show the full transition:

```text
general action statement
→ one concrete case
→ what is known from that case
→ what the case does not prove
```

The example must explicitly show that one concrete case can establish that something happened at least once, but cannot by itself establish frequency, recurrence, causality or a stable pattern.

### Step 4 — Remember one recent, sufficiently well-remembered case

The learner recalls one concrete case where the selected statement really occurred.

Prefer a **recent case that is remembered well enough to identify what happened**, rather than a distant or merely dramatic memory.

The case is usable only when the learner can recover at least:

- approximately when / in what situation it happened;
- what happened;
- what they actually did or did not do;
- the immediate observable result.

For these core identifying elements, `не знаю / не помню` is not a normal completed answer. If the case cannot be recovered sufficiently, choose another case or wait for a naturally occurring future case.

### Step 5 — Record the case in a small card

Approved case-card structure:

1. **Когда / в какой ситуации это было?**
2. **Что тогда происходило?** — only what is genuinely remembered.
3. **Что вы реально сделали или не сделали?**
4. **Что произошло сразу после?**
5. **Был ли до этого конкретный план или намерение? Если да — какое?** — optional; skip when no such plan existed.

The optional intention field preserves a relevant fact where one existed without forcing intention into every case.

### Step 6 — Check whether the case is usable

The learner checks, in simple language:

- Is this one concrete case rather than a week, habit or identity statement?
- Can I approximately identify when / in what situation it happened?
- Do I actually remember what I did or did not do?
- Have I written what happened rather than treating an explanation as fact?
- Am I using this one case as if it already proved «часто / всегда / обычно»?

This is a factual quality gate, not a score.

### Step 7 — See the result and carry the same observation into ordinary life

The learner sees three layers:

```text
initial general statement
→ what one concrete case now establishes
→ what remains open
```

Field practice must not ask only for the next occurrence that **confirms** the initial statement.

Instead:

> **When the next suitable real-life situation occurs in the selected area, preserve what actually happened — whether it confirms, qualifies or contradicts the initial description.**

The field capture remains minimal:

- when / situation;
- what happened;
- what the learner actually did or did not do;
- what happened immediately after.

Do not require all-day monitoring, deliberate creation of difficult situations or behavior change.

## 3. Research-informed refinements accepted with this approval

The approval includes three refinements introduced after additional review of research on autobiographical memory, retrospective self-report, event-contingent / momentary observation, measurement reactivity and instructional design:

1. **Orientation output 3 is provisional.** General statements such as «часто» or «обычно» are not treated as verified behavioral frequencies before reality-based cases accumulate.
2. **The first independent case should preferably be recent and well remembered.** The goal is not memory testing; it is to reduce avoidable retrospective reconstruction.
3. **Field observation must allow disconfirmation.** The next suitable situation is captured regardless of whether the result matches the initial self-description.

These refinements do not change the core seven-step architecture; they tighten its epistemic quality.

## 4. User-language rule

The learner should not need to learn Selection Point terminology in order to perform Selection Point operations.

Prefer ordinary formulations such as:

- «Чего я хочу?»
- «Что есть сейчас?»
- «Что я реально делаю сейчас?»
- «Возьмите один пункт».
- «Вспомните один недавний конкретный случай, который вы хорошо помните».
- «Что тогда происходило?»
- «Что вы реально сделали?»
- «Что произошло сразу после?»

Internal terms such as `episode`, `relevant episode`, `current reality`, `realization`, `reality check`, `observable learning trace` and similar vocabulary remain internal unless a later lesson genuinely requires them.

## 5. Lesson 1 boundary

Lesson 1 still does **not** teach:

- causal reconstruction;
- hidden-cause search;
- need identification;
- alternative-behavior selection;
- participation-point training;
- full feedback-cycle work;
- repeating-pattern / scenario analysis;
- frequency validation from a single case.

Behavior change is not required for Lesson 1 completion.
