# Decision — Orientation Current-Action Layer and Lesson 1 Reframing

**ID:** SP-COURSE-DEC-S1-ENTRY-ACTION-001  
**Date:** 11 October 2026  
**Status:** approved  
**Owner decision:** «Утверждаем»  
**Applies to:** Stage 1 / «Эффективное тело»

## 1. Decision

The Stage 1 entry architecture is refined.

Orientation remains a short standalone self-paced module before the five core lessons, but it now produces **three learner-facing outputs**:

1. **What do I want?** — the participant-owned created result / direction.
2. **What is true now?** — the factual current position relative to that result.
3. **What do I actually do or not do now in this area?** — an initial description of the learner's current realized movement across ordinary days and weeks.

The third output is part of factual current reality, but it must remain distinguishable from the current position itself:

```text
current position
≠
current realized movement / what I actually do or do not do
```

It is not a causal conclusion. Orientation does **not** claim that a listed action is already proven to be the reason the learner has the present result.

The wording «что я делаю каждый день, что имею именно то, что есть сейчас» is therefore treated as a useful design intuition, not as the canonical learner question, because it presupposes causality and daily frequency before they are established.

## 2. Orientation output 3 boundary

The learner may include actions or inactions that:

- appear to support the desired direction;
- appear not to support it;
- are neutral or unclear;
- happen often, sometimes, or only in particular circumstances.

Orientation must not turn this into a list of failures or a weakest-link inventory.

Statements such as «я часто пропускаю тренировку после тяжёлого рабочего дня» are treated as **initial self-descriptions to be grounded in reality**, not yet as verified frequency claims or causal explanations.

Orientation still does not require selecting or analysing a concrete case.

## 3. Revised Lesson 1 function

Lesson 1 no longer begins by selecting an arbitrary relevant part of current reality.

Its first trained operation is now:

> **Take one general statement about what I actually do or do not do now and ground it in one concrete remembered case where this really happened.**

Internal compact form:

```text
created result / direction
+
current position
+
initial description of current action / inaction
→ choose one general action statement
→ locate one concrete remembered case
→ preserve what actually happened
```

A single case can establish that the described event happened at least once. It **cannot by itself prove** that the behavior is frequent, recurring, causal, or representative of the learner's whole trajectory.

## 4. Case eligibility and uncertainty

A remembered case is usable only if the learner can identify enough of the event to know that it is a concrete case rather than a vague reconstruction.

At minimum, the learner must be able to recover:

- approximately when / in what situation it happened;
- what happened;
- what they actually did or did not do;
- the immediate observable result.

For these core identifying elements, `не знаю / не помню` does **not** count as a normal completed answer. If the learner cannot recover enough to identify the case, they choose another remembered case or wait for a naturally occurring future case.

`Не знаю / не наблюдал` remains valid for things that are genuinely unknown, especially:

- why the event happened;
- whether a particular condition caused it;
- unobserved internal or external details;
- whether one case represents a broader pattern.

No cause is invented to complete the exercise.

## 5. User-language rule

The learner-facing course must use the simplest language that preserves the operation.

Internal methodological terms such as `episode`, `relevant episode`, `current reality`, `realization`, `reality check`, `observable learning trace` or similar vocabulary are not automatically exposed to the learner.

Prefer ordinary formulations such as:

- «Чего я хочу?»
- «Что есть сейчас?»
- «Что я реально делаю сейчас?»
- «Вспомните один конкретный случай, когда это произошло».
- «Что тогда произошло?»
- «Что вы реально сделали?»

A learner should not need to learn Selection Point terminology in order to perform a Selection Point operation.

It does not invalidate the five-lesson sequence, self-paced delivery, or the rule that Orientation is not counted as a core lesson.

The Fritz organizing axis remains `created result + factual current reality`; the new third learner-facing output is an explicit movement layer **inside current reality**, not a replacement for that axis.

## 7. Development consequence

The previously approved Lesson 1 brief v3 is reopened for revision because its entry conditions and Lesson 1 operation are no longer fully current.

The next project step is:

> **Rebuild the Lesson 1 learner journey around the approved three-part Orientation entry and present it for owner approval.**

Until that revised journey / brief is approved, writing the final learner-facing Lesson 1 is not authorized.

## 8. Scope boundary

This decision does not authorize:

- Lesson 2 development;
- changing the five-core-lesson sequence;
- Stage 2 / Foundation S5;
- questionnaire redesign;
- external pilot;
- trainer implementation;
- efficacy claims.
