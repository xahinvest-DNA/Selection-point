## 1. Product objective

Create an educational module that can actually teach the first Selection Point stage to a real person through theoretical material, independent practical work and reality-coupled field application.

The module should be understandable without reading Foundation documents or project governance and without a required live interlocutor.

It should contain enough theory to make the mechanism intelligible and enough practice to produce direct lived feedback.

## 1.1. Field and direction

First teaching field:

> **Эффективное тело**

Operational direction:

> **Системно выбирать и фактически совершать действия, которые сохраняют здоровье, повышают физическую эффективность и расширяют возможности тела в будущем.**

Short formulation:

> **Тело со временем становится здоровее, сильнее и способнее.**

The course is not a weight-loss course. Weight may be one indicator; health is the direction; trajectory management is the transferable skill.

## 2. Central course question

> **What must a person understand, notice and practice so that they begin to see the difference between an automatic continuation and an actually available next choice?**

This is a teaching question, not a measurement-system question.

## 3. Required learner outcome

By the end of Stage 1, the learner should have a grounded first-hand understanding that:

- the first reaction/impulse/story is not automatically the only continuation;
- facts, internal reactions and interpretations can be distinguished enough to change what happens next;
- understanding alone is not action;
- a chosen continuation must be realized to meet reality;
- missed or late noticing is data, not moral failure;
- ordinary life supplies practice situations.

The exact wording and sequence remain governed by the approved course structure and current owner decisions.

## 4. Required course components

The Stage 1 course should include:

1. **Opening / hook** — why this matters in ordinary life.
2. **Core model** — simple explanation of automatic continuation and choice.
3. **Examples** — body and other ordinary-life situations where useful.
4. **Primary distinctions** — only those needed by the learner at the current level.
5. **Independent practices** — simple, safe, repeatable exercises that do not require a live guide.
6. **Real-life assignment** — use the operation outside the instructional material.
7. **Reality check / reflection** — extract facts and feedback without turning them into self-judgment.
8. **Common traps** — intellectual understanding, over-analysis, fighting automaticity, “I failed,” retrospective storytelling.
9. **Integration** — what the learner carries into the next stage.
10. **Embedded self-paced support** — explicit criteria, safety boundaries and limited prewritten `if → then` branches for predictable drift; no required teacher/AI correction loop.

The controlling reusable anatomy is defined in `docs/COURSE/STAGE_1_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` v3.

## 5. Course-design criteria

The course is successful as a design if it is:

- simple enough to learn independently;
- deep enough not to become motivational advice;
- reality-coupled;
- non-moralizing;
- practical under ordinary load;
- consistent with Foundation boundaries;
- able to produce an experience the learner can recognize for themselves;
- operational without a required teacher, facilitator or AI assistant.

## 6. What is explicitly out of scope now

Do not make these prerequisites for course creation:

- full observability schema;
- validated measurement scale;
- post-protocol pilot architecture;
- participant scoring;
- questionnaire optimization or redesign;
- Gate closure artifacts;
- trainer/software implementation.

## 7. Research inputs

Use selectively:

### Foundation / canon
Primary source of approved meaning.

### Bill Williams
Development levels, level-appropriate maps/tools, feedback from reality, movement from novice toward expert functioning. Do not import unsupported chaos/brain claims as established psychology.

### Dan Millman
Present-moment contact, body, simple action and practice in ordinary life. Do not use present-moment emphasis to erase trajectory/history.

### Robert Fritz
Created result/direction + factual current reality as the course-wide axis; actions and routes serve the created result rather than replacing it.

### Gate-era Stage 1 research
Reference library only. Extract useful psychological distinctions, evidence and Red Team warnings.

### External scientific research

- what to teach;
- how to teach it;
- which exercise is plausible;
- where a claim needs qualification.
