# Decision — Stage 1 Orientation as a Standalone Self-Paced Module

**Date:** 11 October 2026  
**Status:** approved  
**Owner decision:** approved  
**Project:** Selection Point  
**Applies to:** Stage 1 / «Эффективное тело»

## Decision

Orientation is a **standalone short self-paced module** that precedes the five core Stage 1 lessons.

Orientation is **not counted as one of the five lessons**.

Its role is to establish the learner's coordinate system before Lesson 1:

```text
participant-owned created result / direction
+
factual current reality relative to that result
```

Orientation does not select or analyse a concrete episode. Episode work begins in Lesson 1.

## Binding implications

1. The five-core-lesson structure remains unchanged.
2. Lesson 1 receives the Orientation outputs as its starting coordinates; it does not recreate them from scratch.
3. Orientation must be able to function without a teacher, facilitator or required AI assistant.
4. Orientation should remain short and bounded: it establishes coordinates rather than teaching the full Stage 1 mechanism.
5. Orientation does not open causal reconstruction, hidden-cause search, behavior-change training, repeating-pattern analysis or Stage 2 work.
6. Learner-facing Orientation content and the complete Lesson 1 learner journey remain separate design artifacts and require their own review before being treated as approved content.

## Scope boundary

This decision approves only the **role and format position** of Orientation in the course architecture.

It does not by itself approve:

- the final learner-facing text or screen sequence of Orientation;
- the full Lesson 1 learner journey previously discussed;
- Lesson 2 development;
- a change to the five-lesson sequence;
- questionnaire redesign;
- Stage 2 / Foundation S5;
- external pilot or trainer implementation.
