## Decision

Approve `docs/COURSE/STAGE_1_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` version 3 as the controlling reusable lesson architecture for the five Stage 1 / «Эффективное тело» lessons.

The course remains a **self-paced online course**. Its core delivery must not depend on a teacher, facilitator or required AI assistant.

## Binding architecture

Each lesson is a learning and reality-interaction cycle organized around:

```text
created direction + current reality
→ one new capacity
→ minimum level-appropriate map
→ worked example
→ independent application
→ factual reality check
→ application in ordinary life
→ realized action / observation
→ consequence / reality response
→ updated current reality
```

Field practice is part of the lesson itself rather than optional homework.

The lesson must distinguish understanding, selection and realization. Desired outcome is not required; actual contact between a realized continuation and reality is required for the learning cycle.

Each lesson leaves an observable learning trace, but form completion is not itself evidence of learning.

## Methodological synthesis

The approved design uses the three source traditions as design inputs rather than as one merged theory:

- **Robert Fritz:** participant-owned created direction + factual current reality; skills/actions are means rather than the primary direction.
- **Dan Millman:** ordinary life as the practice arena, present reality as the point of action, and action rather than waiting for a preferred internal state.
- **Bill Williams:** developmental discipline and level-appropriate maps/tools; Stage 1 does not import global underlying-structure diagnosis or later-stage pattern analysis.

Selection Point retains its own boundaries: fact ≠ interpretation; unknown is valid; late noticing is valid; direction ≠ route ≠ next step ≠ realized action; selection ≠ realization; reality feedback has priority over explanatory elegance.
