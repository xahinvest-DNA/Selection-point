# Stage 1 — Reusable Lesson Template

**ID:** SP-COURSE-S1-TEMPLATE-001

**Date:** 28 September 2026

**Field:** «Эффективное тело»

## 1. Purpose

This template defines the stable delivery mechanics for all five Stage 1 lessons before individual lesson scripts are written.

Core rule:

    real experience
    → one new distinction
    → guided application
    → one real-world task
    → reality
    → next debrief

A lesson is incomplete if it produces only insight, agreement, motivation, a plan or a promise.

## 2. Course-wide axis

Every lesson remains anchored to:

1. **Created direction** — what the participant wants to create in relation to the body.
2. **Current reality** — what is factually true now.

Working relation:

    created direction
    +
    current reality
    →
    relevant next learning/action

The direction is participant-owned and revisable after new reality.

## 3. Standard seven-phase lesson

Recommended active session: 25–40 minutes.

### Phase 1 — Reality return / debrief

Target: 3–7 minutes.

Primary question:

> **Что фактически произошло после прошлого занятия?**

Clarify only as needed:
- what was intended;
- what was actually done;
- what happened afterward;
- what remains unknown.

Do not begin with «Получилось?».

If no relevant episode occurred, do not manufacture one. Use an earlier real episode or a neutral body-domain example.

### Phase 2 — Worked example

Target: 4–7 minutes.

Before asking the learner to perform a new operation, show it on one clear example.

The example must be:
- concrete;
- short;
- body-domain relevant;
- non-moralizing;
- free of certainty about hidden causes.

It must also show why the habitual action made local sense.

Bad:

> «Он устал и глупо купил алкоголь».

Better:

> «После тяжёлого дня алкоголь обещал быстро изменить состояние. Это объясняет его локальную привлекательность, но не делает его обязательным продолжением».

Only today's new operation is demonstrated.

### Phase 3 — One explicit distinction

Target: 4–8 minutes.

Teach one central distinction only.

Structure:
1. familiar compressed view;
2. new distinction;
3. why it matters;
4. one boundary/counterexample.

Maximum:
- one primary model;
- three supporting terms;
- one diagram.

If a concept does not change the learner's operation in this lesson, move it to teacher notes or remove it.

### Phase 4 — Guided application to the learner's episode

Target: 8–15 minutes.

This is the center of the lesson.

Rule:

> **Teacher/AI holds the structure. Learner supplies the content.**

Ask one question at a time, not a block of 8–12 questions.

Typical hierarchy:
- Что конкретно произошло?
- В каком состоянии ты был?
- Как ситуация выглядела для тебя тогда?
- Что действие должно было изменить прямо сейчас?
- Что оно фактически дало сразу?
- Что произошло потом?
- Что ты действительно заметил тогда, а не только сейчас?

Keep distinguishable:
- fact;
- report of inner experience;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis;
- unknown.

Teacher/AI may offer a hypothesis only as a hypothesis:

> «Одна из версий — действие быстро снижало напряжение. Похоже или нет?»

Never:

> «Ты сделал это потому, что на самом деле...»

### Phase 5 — Present-action bridge

Target: 1–5 minutes.

Before ending analysis, ask:

> **После этого разбора есть ли один небольшой реальный шаг, который имеет смысл сделать сейчас?**

Possible outcome:
- yes — do or prepare it now;
- no — do not force action;
- unsafe/inappropriate — do not act.

Examples:
- prepare food or water;
- prepare equipment;
- send a support request;
- stop one further continuation;
- prepare tomorrow's first physical step.

Purpose: prevent analysis from becoming the endpoint.

### Phase 6 — One field assignment

Target: 2–4 minutes.

Every lesson gives one task only.

Good form:

    WHEN a relevant episode occurs,
    NOTICE/DO one specific thing,
    CAPTURE one minimal fact.

Bad:
- «будь осознаннее всю неделю»;
- «следи за собой постоянно»;
- «не допускай срывов».

Field capture target: 30–90 seconds.

The daily trajectory questionnaire remains separate.

### Phase 7 — Neutral close

Target: under 1 minute.

State:
- what is being tested;
- what is not required;
- what to bring back.

Example:

> «До следующего раза задача не в том, чтобы всегда поступать иначе. Нужен один реальный эпизод, где сегодняшнее различение стало применимо — или где стало видно, что не стало».

Avoid motivational inflation and praise based only on a desirable outcome.

## 4. Compact learner artifact per lesson

Orientation:
- created direction;
- current reality;
- one relevant episode.

Lesson 1:
- one bounded episode.

Lesson 2:
- one reconstructed chain.

Lesson 3:
- state separated from familiar regulation means and immediate effect.

Lesson 4:
- one genuinely available participation point;
- one continuation serving the created direction.

Lesson 5:
- selected → realized → consequence → updated current reality → next move.

These artifacts are reviewed together during integration.

## 5. Teacher / AI modes

Use four modes deliberately.

### Explain
Short, concrete, example-led. Use only when the operation is not understood.

### Ask
Default mode in guided application. One question at a time.

### Reflect
Compress the learner's material without adding theory.

Example:

> «Факт: после длинного рабочего дня была сильная усталость; алкоголь дал быстрое ощущение выключения. Верно?»

### Reality-check
Challenge claims stronger than the evidence.

Learner:

> «У меня вообще не было выбора».

Teacher:

> «Мы можем точно сказать, что другого хода ты тогда не увидел. Можем ли мы из этого эпизода установить, что другого хода объективно не существовало?»

The purpose is epistemic accuracy, not argument.

## 6. Adaptive branches

### «Я ничего не помню»
Do not force reconstruction. Preserve last known fact and unknown links.

### «Я всё понял, но ничего не сделал»
Do not repeat theory. Move to selected vs realized and what was actually available.

### «У меня не было выбора»
Do not insist there was. Distinguish:
- did not see another move;
- could not perform another move;
- another move did not exist.

### «Я снова сорвался»
Do not reset the course. Find where noticing occurred, what remains open now, what can be repaired, or what next-cycle condition can change.

### «Это слишком очевидно»
Move immediately to a real episode and test whether the learner can actually perform the operation.

### «Я хочу понять глубокую причину»
Keep the question, but do not let it block Stage 1. One episode and available participation remain the current object.

## 7. Field-practice rules

- one operation at a time;
- natural recurrence, not manufactured stress;
- late entry is valid;
- action must fit current reality and created direction;
- no requirement for perfect early noticing;
- no requirement to eliminate automaticity;
- no global self-monitoring.

## 8. Questionnaire separation

    DAILY QUESTIONNAIRE
    = longitudinal telemetry

    COURSE LESSON
    = skill acquisition

Questionnaire data may supply a candidate episode.

Lesson questions do not automatically become questionnaire fields.

This preserves longitudinal comparability and avoids turning measurement into the whole training method.

## 9. Safety boundary

The course is educational, not medical treatment.

Teacher/AI must not:
- diagnose dependence, eating disorders or psychiatric conditions;
- recommend abrupt withdrawal from potentially dependent substance use;
- override medical restrictions;
- encourage introspection during dangerous physical work, driving or time-critical skilled activity.

Acute medical/safety issues override ordinary course flow.

## 10. Tone

Preferred:
- concrete;
- neutral;
- precise;
- ordinary language;
- no mystification;
- no inflated praise.

Avoid:
- «подсознание решило»;
- «тело знает правильный ответ»;
- «ты сам это выбрал» when availability is not established;
- «просто сделай»;
- personality labels presented as facts.

## 11. Lesson-quality checklist

Before approving a lesson script:

1. Does it add one identifiable operation?
2. Is there one clear worked example?
3. Does the learner apply it to their own episode?
4. Is created direction visible without coercion?
5. Is current reality preserved without moral judgment?
6. Are fact, interpretation and hypothesis distinguishable?
7. Is Stage 2 pattern analysis excluded?
8. Is there a present-action bridge?
9. Is there only one field assignment?
10. Can field capture be done in 30–90 seconds?
11. Is next debrief obvious?
12. Is questionnaire telemetry separate?
13. Is success defined as learning/participation, not body outcome?
14. Is unknown allowed?
15. Is late noticing valid?

Any “no” requires revision.

## 12. Stable vs variable

Stable across all five lessons:

    reality return
    → worked example
    → one distinction
    → guided self-application
    → present-action bridge
    → field task
    → next debrief

Variable:
- central distinction;
- worked example;
- guided questions;
- learner artifact;
- field assignment;
- completion signal.
