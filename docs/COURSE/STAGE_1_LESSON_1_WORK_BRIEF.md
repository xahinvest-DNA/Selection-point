## 1. Purpose

Define the controlling methodological and product boundary for writing the new learner-facing Lesson 1.

The current entry architecture is:

```text
ORIENTATION
1. Чего я хочу?
2. Что есть сейчас?
3. Что я реально делаю или не делаю сейчас в этой области?

→ LESSON 1
one general action / inaction statement
→ one concrete remembered case
→ factual grounding without overclaiming
```

Orientation output 3 is an **initial general self-description of current movement**. It is not yet a verified frequency claim, recurring pattern or causal explanation.

## 2. Entry conditions

Lesson 1 begins only after the standalone Orientation module has produced three things.

### 2.1 Participant-owned desired result / direction

Learner-facing formulation:

> **Чего я хочу?**

### 2.2 Factual current position relative to that result

Learner-facing formulation:

> **Что есть сейчас?**

### 2.3 Initial description of current realized movement

Learner-facing formulation:

> **Что я реально делаю или не делаю сейчас в этой области?**

This may include useful, unwanted, neutral or unclear actions / inactions.

Statements containing words such as `часто`, `обычно`, `постоянно`, `редко` are treated as the learner's initial general description, not as established behavioral frequency.

Orientation does **not** need to supply a concrete case.

## 3. One main learning operation

> **Take one general statement about what I actually do or do not do now and ground it in one concrete remembered case where this really happened.**

Compact internal form:

```text
desired result / direction
+
current position
+
initial general action / inaction statement
→ choose one statement
→ remember one concrete case where it happened
→ preserve what actually happened
```

Learner-facing formulation should remain simple:

> **Вспомните один недавний конкретный случай, когда это произошло и который вы достаточно хорошо помните.**

The concrete case is a means of grounding an initial self-description in reality. It is not the organizing goal of the course.

## 4. Why this operation belongs first

Orientation may produce compressed statements such as:

- «я часто пропускаю тренировку после тяжёлого рабочего дня»;
- «я обычно ложусь поздно»;
- «если готовлю всё заранее, утром чаще тренируюсь»;
- «в выходные я двигаюсь меньше».

These statements may be useful starting descriptions, but they combine multiple days, memories and implicit conclusions.

Lesson 1 teaches the learner to connect one such statement to one identifiable real case.

A single case establishes only that the event happened at least once. It does **not** establish:

- actual frequency;
- a repeating pattern;
- causality;
- representativeness of the whole person or trajectory.

Lesson 1 does not yet teach why the event happened or how to change it.

## 5. Minimum map

The lesson needs only the distinctions required for this operation.

### 5.1 Current position ≠ current realized movement

What is true about the learner's present state is not identical to what they actually do or do not do across ordinary life.

Both belong to current reality internally, but they should not be silently collapsed.

### 5.2 General action statement ≠ one concrete case

```text
«я часто / обычно / постоянно делаю X»
≠
one identifiable case where X actually happened
```

### 5.3 What happened ≠ explanation of why it happened

```text
«я не начал тренировку»
≠
«я не начал, потому что ленивый»
```

### 5.4 One case ≠ proven frequency

One concrete case can support:

> **«Так действительно произошло хотя бы один раз».**

It cannot by itself support:

> **«Так происходит всегда / обычно / часто».**

No additional psychological model is required in Lesson 1.

## 6. Approved seven-step learner journey

The learner-facing Lesson 1 must preserve this sequence.

### Step 1 — Return to the three Orientation outputs

Show the learner:

- what they want;
- what is true now;
- what they currently describe themselves as actually doing or not doing.

Do not recreate Orientation work.

### Step 2 — Choose one general statement

The learner chooses one item from Orientation output 3 to examine more closely.

The selected item may describe useful, neutral or unwanted action / inaction.

Do not ask for «the main problem» or default to the weakest behavior.

### Step 3 — Show one complete worked example

Before independent application, show the transition:

```text
general action statement
→ one concrete case
→ what the case establishes
→ what the case does not establish
```

The example must make the limit of one case explicit.

### Step 4 — Remember one recent, sufficiently well-remembered case

Prefer a recent case that the learner remembers well enough to identify what happened.

The learner should not choose a distant memory merely because it is dramatic.

If the selected case cannot be recovered sufficiently, choose another case or wait for a naturally occurring future case.

### Step 5 — Fill the small case card

Use the approved case-card structure in section 8.

### Step 6 — Check whether the case is usable

Use the factual quality gate in section 10.

### Step 7 — See the result and carry observation into ordinary life

Show the learner:

```text
initial general statement
→ what this one case now establishes
→ what remains open
```

Then give the field task in section 11.

## 7. Case-selection rule and eligibility

The learner selects one statement from Orientation output 3 and remembers one concrete case where the described action or inaction really occurred.

Prefer a **recent case that is sufficiently well remembered**.

Selection must not default to:

- the worst lapse;
- the most shameful behavior;
- the strongest emotional event;
- alcohol or another salient weak point;
- whatever seems easiest to moralize.

Useful, neutral and unwanted actions are all admissible.

A case is usable only when the learner can recover enough to identify it, at minimum:

- approximately when / in what situation it happened;
- what happened;
- what they actually did or did not do;
- the immediate observable result.

For these core event-identifying elements, `не знаю / не помню` does **not** count as a normal completed answer.

If the learner cannot remember enough, the correct continuation is:

- choose another remembered case; or
- wait for a naturally occurring future case and capture it then.

`Не знаю / не наблюдал` remains valid for information that is genuinely unknown, especially:

- why it happened;
- whether a particular condition caused it;
- unobserved internal or external details;
- whether one case represents a broader pattern.

The lesson must not force the learner to invent a cause or missing detail.

## 8. Approved case card

The card remains deliberately small.

Required fields:

1. **Когда / в какой ситуации это было?**
2. **Что тогда происходило?** — only what is genuinely remembered.
3. **Что вы реально сделали или не сделали?**
4. **Что произошло сразу после?**

Optional field:

5. **Был ли до этого конкретный план или намерение? Если да — какое?**

The intention field is optional because intention is relevant only when it actually existed. The lesson must not manufacture an intention merely to complete the form.

## 9. Worked example requirement

Before independent application, show one ordinary body-domain example.

The example should demonstrate:

```text
Orientation output-3 statement
→ one recent concrete case
→ minimum factual description
→ what is established
→ what remains unknown / unproven
```

Prefer low-drama material such as:

- planned movement or training;
- sleep / recovery;
- food organization;
- physical workload;
- preparation for a body-related action;
- an ordinary action that was actually realized.

The example should not imply that the course is primarily about failure prevention.

The example must explicitly show that one case does not prove a recurring pattern or causal explanation.

## 10. Factual quality gate

The learner checks, in simple language:

- Is this one concrete case rather than a description of a whole week, habit or identity?
- Can I say approximately when / in what situation it happened?
- Do I actually remember what I did or did not do?
- Have I written what happened rather than turning an explanation into fact?
- Am I treating one case as if it already proved «часто / всегда / обычно»?

This is a factual quality gate, not a score.

If the case cannot be identified, the correct continuation is to choose another case or wait for a naturally occurring case, not to complete the form with invented memory.

## 11. Field practice

Field practice is the second part of Lesson 1.

The task is **not** to wait specifically for another event that confirms the learner's initial statement.

Instead:

> **Когда в выбранной области возникнет следующая подходящая ситуация, сохраните, что произошло на самом деле — независимо от того, подтвердило это ваше первоначальное описание, уточнило его или ему противоречило.**

The field capture should remain minimal:

- when / situation;
- what happened;
- what the learner actually did or did not do;
- what happened immediately after.

Capture should occur close enough to the event to reduce avoidable retrospective reconstruction, when practical and safe.

Do not require the learner to:

- prevent the first reaction;
- change behavior;
- choose a better alternative;
- interrupt automaticity;
- observe themselves continuously all day;
- create a difficult situation deliberately.

The purpose is to let reality confirm, qualify or contradict the initial general description.

## 12. Observable learning trace

At Lesson 1 completion, the learner should possess:

1. the Orientation outputs:
   - desired result / direction;
   - factual current position;
   - initial general description of current action / inaction;
2. one identifiable concrete case connected to one of those action statements;
3. a clear distinction between what the case establishes and what it does not establish;
4. one additional field case if a suitable situation naturally occurs before the next lesson.

The minimum mandatory Lesson 1 artifact is **one usable concrete case**.

## 13. Lesson 2 handoff

Lesson 1 stops once a case is sufficiently concrete and factual.

Lesson 2 receives that case and asks a different question about how the continuation was composed from distinguishable local links.

Therefore Lesson 1 must **not** perform:

- causal reconstruction;
- hidden-cause search;
- need identification;
- full state-regulation analysis;
- selection of alternative behavior;
- participation-point training;
- consequence-feedback analysis of a changed action;
- repeating-pattern / scenario analysis;
- frequency validation from a single case.

## 14. User-language rule

Learner-facing language must be simpler than the internal methodological language whenever the term is not needed to perform the next action.

Do not require the learner to understand terms such as:

- `episode` / «эпизод»;
- `релевантный эпизод`;
- `current reality` / «текущая реальность» as technical vocabulary;
- `реализация`;
- `reality check`;
- `observable learning trace`;
- `functional chain`.

Prefer ordinary formulations such as:

- «Чего я хочу?»
- «Что есть сейчас?»
- «Что я реально делаю сейчас?»
- «Возьмите один пункт».
- «Вспомните один недавний конкретный случай, который вы хорошо помните».
- «Что тогда происходило?»
- «Что вы реально сделали?»
- «Что произошло сразу после?»

The methodological layer may remain precise internally. The learner should not need to learn the vocabulary of Selection Point in order to use it.

## 15. Williams / Millman / Fritz alignment

### Fritz

The course remains organized by:

```text
created result
+
factual current reality
```

The explicit current-action layer is treated as part of current reality, not as a replacement for the Fritz axis and not as a causal claim made in advance.

### Millman

The learner moves from generalized self-story to ordinary lived reality. Field work uses naturally occurring life and allows reality to contradict the initial description.

Lesson 1 does not force action change before the learner has learned to see what actually happened.

### Williams

The learner receives one level-appropriate tool: move from a broad action statement to one concrete case.

The lesson does not introduce broader underlying-structure or Stage 2 pattern analysis.

## 16. Safety and epistemic boundaries

- fact ≠ interpretation;
- one case ≠ verified frequency or recurring pattern;
- the learner does not invent memory to complete a form;
- core identifying facts must be remembered well enough for the case to be usable;
- unknown remains valid for causes and genuinely unobserved details;
- field observation must permit confirmation, qualification or contradiction of the initial self-description;
- no hidden cause is assigned as fact;
- no worst-failure selection rule;
- no permanent attention to the weakest behavior;
- no unsafe or deliberately provocative field task;
- appropriate support may override the exercise;
- no constant all-day self-monitoring.

## 17. Approved acceptance criteria

Owner approval confirms that:

1. Orientation supplies three outputs: desired result, current position and initial general action / inaction description.
2. Output 3 remains provisional rather than verified frequency or causality.
3. Lesson 1 develops exactly one main operation: grounding one general action / inaction statement in one concrete case.
4. The first independent case should preferably be recent and sufficiently well remembered.
5. Core event-identifying information cannot be replaced by `не знаю / не помню`.
6. The approved case card uses four required fields plus one optional prior-plan / intention field.
7. One case cannot prove `часто / всегда / обычно`.
8. The learner journey uses seven steps defined in section 6.
9. Field observation captures the next suitable situation regardless of whether it confirms or contradicts the initial description.
10. Learner-facing language remains simple and does not require Selection Point terminology.
11. Lesson 1 requires no behavior change and stops before functional-chain reconstruction.
12. The course remains self-paced and preserves the five-core-lesson sequence.
