**Course:** Stage 1 / «Эффективное тело»  
**Lesson:** 1 — «Один эпизод вместо “я такой”»  
**Delivery:** self-paced online course; no required teacher, facilitator or AI assistant  
**Template:** `docs/COURSE/STAGE_1_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` v3

## 1. Purpose of this brief

Define the exact methodological and delivery boundary for Lesson 1 before a new learner-facing lesson is written.

This brief translates the approved self-paced template v3 into Lesson 1 requirements. It does not itself contain the final lesson copy.

## 2. Single learning operation

Lesson 1 develops one operation only:

> **Replace a global conclusion about the person or their life with one bounded, reality-anchored episode that can actually be examined.**

Compact form:

```text
global label / vague problem
→ one concrete episode
→ minimum factual description
→ current position becomes more distinguishable
```

The learner is not yet being taught to change the behavior inside the episode.

The target capacity is to make one episode sufficiently concrete and bounded that it can later be reconstructed in Lesson 2.

## 3. Position inside the course-wide axis

Lesson 1 remains inside:

```text
participant-owned created body direction
+
factual current reality
→
which concrete episode is relevant to examine?
```

The episode is selected because it matters relative to the participant's created body direction, not because it is the worst failure, most shameful behavior or most emotionally charged event.

The skill of episode localization is a means. It is not the body direction itself.

## 4. Entry condition from Orientation

Orientation is expected to provide three inputs:

1. a participant-owned created body direction;
2. a factual current-reality description;
3. one recent episode that appears relevant to that direction.

Lesson 1 should use that episode when it is suitable.

If the Orientation episode is too broad, too vague, unavailable in memory, or not meaningfully relevant, the learner may choose another ordinary recent episode without treating the change as failure.

The lesson must not require the learner to find a dramatic lapse.

## 5. Minimum map / distinction

The central distinction is:

```text
global conclusion about me / my life
≠
one concrete episode
```

Examples of global conclusions:

- «Я ленивый»;
- «У меня нет режима»;
- «Я постоянно переедаю»;
- «Я всегда всё бросаю»;
- «После тяжёлой работы я обязательно срываюсь».

The lesson should show that such statements may point toward something important, but they are too compressed and global to examine directly.

The learner is invited to move from the global statement to one event that can be placed in ordinary reality.

A minimal supporting distinction is allowed:

```text
what was observed / remembered
≠
what I explain or conclude about it
```

This distinction is used only to keep the episode factual enough for Lesson 1. It must not expand into full causal reconstruction.

## 6. Required episode content

The final bounded episode should contain only the minimum needed for later use:

1. **what happened** — a short event description;
2. **when / where** — enough to locate the event in time and context;
3. **relevant factual conditions** — only conditions that are actually known and needed to understand the event;
4. **realized action / inaction** — what was actually done or not done;
5. **immediate observable result** — what factually followed immediately or what position existed at the end of the episode.

`Unknown / I do not remember` is valid wherever detail is unavailable.

The learner must not invent missing information merely to complete the form.

## 7. Worked-example requirement

Before independent work, the lesson must show one clear body-domain example.

The example should demonstrate the transformation itself:

```text
global statement
→ selected concrete episode
→ five-field factual record
```

The example should be ordinary and low-drama where possible.

Suitable domains include:

- food;
- sleep;
- fatigue;
- training;
- recovery;
- physical workload;
- planning;
- an ordinary health-related choice.

The example must not teach Lesson 2's full chain or offer a hidden cause.

### Example design pattern

Input:

> «У меня вообще нет режима».

Transformation:

> Choose one recent occurrence that makes this statement feel true.

Output shape:

- Tuesday evening after returning home;
- planned physical activity did not occur;
- the learner spent the next period on another activity;
- bedtime occurred later than planned;
- immediate observable result: planned activity was not completed and the evening ended at a different time than intended.

The finished learner-facing example should use only details that are explicitly presented as facts in the example.

## 8. Independent application

The learner applies the same operation to their own material without a live teacher or AI.

The practical form should contain two layers.

### Layer A — input to be decompressed

One short global or vague statement the learner currently uses about the body, behavior or routine.

This is temporary input, not the final artifact.

### Layer B — one bounded episode

The learner fills the five required episode fields:

1. what happened;
2. when / where;
3. relevant known factual conditions;
4. realized action / inaction;
5. immediate observable result.

The form should be short enough that completion does not become a separate monitoring project.

## 9. Reality-check criteria

The self-paced lesson must provide explicit criteria so the learner can check the episode without external correction.

A valid Lesson 1 episode should satisfy most of the following:

- it describes **one event**, not a week, month, relationship, lifestyle or personality;
- it can be located approximately in time and context;
- it contains what actually happened, not only a general judgment;
- action / inaction is stated as realized fact;
- the immediate result is observable or honestly marked unknown;
- interpretations are not silently presented as facts;
- missing details are allowed to remain unknown;
- the learner has not begun searching for the hidden cause or recurring scenario.

The purpose of the check is factual discrimination, not scoring the learner.

## 10. Prewritten self-paced branches

The lesson should include a small number of explicit `if → then` branches where they prevent predictable drift.

### If the learner still writes a global statement

**Then:** ask for the most recent or clearest single occurrence that makes the statement feel relevant.

### If the episode covers several days or repeated events

**Then:** narrow to one occurrence from beginning to immediate result.

### If exact details are not remembered

**Then:** keep only remembered facts and mark the rest unknown. Do not reconstruct certainty from guesswork.

### If the learner starts explaining why it happened

**Then:** park the explanation for later and return to: what happened, what was done, and what immediately followed.

### If the learner chooses only the worst or most shameful episode

**Then:** explicitly permit a simpler ordinary episode that is relevant to the created direction.

### If no new “problem episode” is available

**Then:** a successful, neutral or mixed episode may be used if it is relevant to the direction. Lesson 1 is about localization, not failure collection.

### If the situation is unsafe, highly distressing or requires professional help

**Then:** do not intensify or recreate it for the course. Choose a safer ordinary episode and seek appropriate support where needed.

## 11. Field practice — second half of Lesson 1

The field task trains the same operation and nothing more.

Task:

> **In ordinary life, capture one naturally occurring body-relevant episode as a bounded factual event.**

The learner is not required to:

- stop the behavior;
- replace it with an alternative;
- find a choice point;
- diagnose a need;
- discover a hidden cause;
- monitor themselves continuously;
- provoke a lapse so there is something to record.

If a relevant event occurs during activity where interruption would be unsafe or impractical, the learner records it later from memory rather than interrupting the activity.

If no new relevant episode occurs before the learner is ready for Lesson 2, the bounded episode created during Lesson 1 may be carried forward. The learner does not invent or provoke an event merely to satisfy the course.

## 12. Realization in Lesson 1

For this lesson, the realized training operation is **episode localization and capture**, not behavior change.

Keep separate:

```text
noticed a broad problem
≠
selected one occurrence
≠
actually produced a bounded episode record
```

The learner-facing lesson should make clear that understanding the distinction without producing an episode is not yet completion of the current operation.

## 13. Reality response / updated position

After the field capture, the learner returns to factual current reality.

The review should remain minimal:

- What episode was actually captured?
- What action / inaction actually occurred?
- What immediate result was observed?
- What is factually true now that was previously hidden inside a global statement?

Do not ask whether the learner “fixed” the issue.

Do not infer a stable pattern from one episode.

A useful update may be as small as:

> «У меня не “всегда так”; сейчас у меня есть один конкретный эпизод, который я могу исследовать дальше.»

## 14. Observable learning trace

The minimum retained result of Lesson 1 is:

> **one bounded body-relevant episode record that is factual enough to become the input for Lesson 2.**

The trace includes the five episode fields and preserves unknowns.

The temporary global statement may be retained for contrast, but it is not required as the core artifact.

The daily trajectory questionnaire is not part of this lesson and is not required to produce the trace.

## 15. Completion signal

Lesson 1 is ready to hand off to Lesson 2 when the learner can take at least one global or vague formulation and produce one bounded episode without requiring a live facilitator to perform the narrowing for them.

This is a **lesson completion signal**, not evidence of stable mastery, independent generalization or course efficacy.

## 16. Interface to Lesson 2

Lesson 2 receives the bounded episode as its input.

Lesson 2 may then introduce a functionally connected local chain.

Lesson 1 must not pre-fill that chain.

The handoff is therefore:

```text
Lesson 1 output:
one bounded episode

→ Lesson 2 input:
the same episode becomes material for local reconstruction
```

## 17. Explicit scope boundary

Lesson 1 must stop before:

- full causal reconstruction;
- hidden-cause search;
- repeating-pattern / scenario analysis;
- global underlying-structure diagnosis;
- body-state versus regulation-method teaching;
- alternative-behavior design;
- participation-point training;
- selected-versus-realized action cycle as a behavioral intervention;
- consequence-as-feedback cycle;
- Stage 2 pattern work.

The lesson may record the action that actually occurred and its immediate result because these are part of factual episode localization. It must not yet teach how to change the continuation.

## 18. Safety and uncertainty requirements

The final lesson must embed the following directly in learner-facing material:

- do not deliberately create a risky or unhealthy episode for practice;
- do not interrupt safety-critical or skilled physical activity for introspection or note-taking;
- use later reconstruction when live capture is unsafe or impractical;
- physical limits and appropriate medical/professional support take priority over course exercises;
- unknown and incomplete memory are valid;
- the lesson does not diagnose medical or psychological causes.

## 19. Acceptance criteria for the future Lesson 1 artifact

A new `STAGE_1_LESSON_1.md` may be considered structurally compliant only if it:

1. works without a teacher or required AI assistant;
2. develops only episode localization;
3. begins from created direction + factual current reality;
4. includes one minimum map and one worked example;
5. includes bounded independent application;
6. includes factual reality-check criteria;
7. treats field practice as the second half of the lesson;
8. does not require behavior change as Lesson 1 success;
9. leaves one bounded episode as the observable trace;
10. carries that episode forward into Lesson 2;
11. preserves unknowns and fact/interpretation separation;
12. embeds safety boundaries;
13. does not leak Lesson 2–5 or Stage 2 operations into Lesson 1.

## 20. Authorization boundary

Approval of this work brief would authorize preparation of a new learner-facing Lesson 1 artifact under template v3.

It would not authorize:

- Lesson 2 development;
- Stage 2 or Foundation S5;
- external pilot execution;
- trainer implementation;
- questionnaire redesign;
- claims of course efficacy.
