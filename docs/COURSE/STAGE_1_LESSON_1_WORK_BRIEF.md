**Controls:** `STAGE_1_EFFECTIVE_BODY_COURSE_STRUCTURE.md`, `STAGE_1_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` v2  
**Purpose:** define the bounded teaching task for Lesson 1 before writing the learner-facing lesson script.

## 1. Lesson role in the course

Lesson 1 introduces the first operational move of the course:

> **Replace a global conclusion about the person or their life with one bounded, reality-anchored episode that can actually be examined.**

The lesson does not yet teach the full automatic-process chain, hidden causes, pattern recognition, alternative regulation strategies or the complete Selection Point cycle.

Its job is narrower:

```text
global label / vague problem
→ one concrete episode
→ minimum factual description
→ current position becomes more distinguishable
```

This creates usable material for Lesson 2 without prematurely teaching Lesson 2.

## 2. Entry condition

The participant should already have completed Orientation sufficiently to have:

1. a participant-owned body direction;
2. a factual current position at a useful level of detail;
3. at least one recent body-domain episode that matters relative to that direction.

Typical entry statements:

- «Я ленивый»;
- «У меня нет дисциплины»;
- «Я постоянно переедаю»;
- «Я никак не могу наладить сон»;
- «Я всё время срываю тренировки»;
- «После тяжёлого дня я уже ни на что не способен».

These statements may contain useful information, but they are too broad to be the object of Stage 1 practice.

## 3. Teaching objective

By the end of Lesson 1 the learner should be able to take one broad self-description or recurring complaint and convert it into one bounded event with enough factual structure that another person could understand what actually occurred without needing a personality explanation.

Compact learner-facing objective:

> **Не объяснить себя целиком. Увидеть один реальный эпизод достаточно точно.**

## 4. The one new distinction

Primary distinction:

```text
global conclusion about me / my life
≠
one concrete episode
```

Examples:

```text
«Я безвольный»
≠
«Во вторник после работы я приехал домой около 20:30, тренировку не начал и лёг смотреть телефон»

«У меня нет режима»
≠
«Вчера я планировал лечь в 23:00, но после 22:30 продолжил рабочую переписку и лёг около 01:10»
```

The lesson does not require deciding which explanation is true.

## 5. Minimum anatomy of a bounded episode

A usable Lesson 1 episode should contain only the minimum needed to establish its boundaries:

1. **What happened?** — one observable event or continuation.
2. **When / where?** — enough context to locate the event.
3. **What relevant conditions were already true?** — only obvious facts that materially locate the current position, such as sleep, workload, time pressure, pain, available support or competing demand.
4. **What was actually done or not done?** — realized action / inaction, not intended action.
5. **What immediate observable result followed?** — the first factual consequence, without building the full causal chain.

Unknown remains valid.

Do not require:
- the hidden reason;
- the true need;
- the full emotional history;
- the repeating pattern;
- the exact lost choice point;
- the correct alternative action.

## 6. Episode-selection rule

Select the episode because it is **relevant to the created body direction**, not because it is the most shameful, dramatic or damaging event.

Good candidates are ordinary and specific:
- sleep and bedtime;
- food quantity or timing;
- recovery after physical work;
- training started, shortened, postponed or skipped;
- asking for help or not asking;
- continuing work despite fatigue;
- preparing or not preparing something needed for the next day.

No single weakness is the default course example.

The selected episode does not need to be a failure. A successful continuation can be equally useful if it reveals what actually happened.

## 7. Worked example for Lesson 1

Preferred example domain: **sleep / late work communication**.

Initial compressed statement:

> «У меня вообще нет режима сна».

Bounded version:

> «Вчера я собирался лечь около 23:00. В 22:35 пришло рабочее сообщение, я открыл переписку и продолжал отвечать примерно до 00:40. После этого ещё около 20 минут смотрел телефон и лёг около 01:05. Утром встал в 06:30 и чувствовал сильную сонливость».

What the example demonstrates:
- the person is not reduced to a label;
- the episode has a beginning and end;
- intended action and realized action can differ;
- relevant load can be named without explaining hidden motives;
- an immediate consequence can be observed;
- no full causal reconstruction is required yet.

Do **not** continue in Lesson 1 into:
- why the message felt urgent;
- what need the phone met;
- which alternative route was best;
- whether this repeats as a pattern.

Those belong to later work.

## 8. Seven-phase delivery mapping

### Phase 1 — Reality return / debrief

Because this is the first core lesson, return to Orientation rather than a previous field assignment.

Primary questions, one at a time:

1. «Какое направление для тела ты сформулировал?»
2. «Что из текущей реальности сейчас наиболее важно не потерять?»
3. «Какой недавний эпизод имеет отношение к этому направлению?»

Do not begin by asking whether the learner succeeded.

### Phase 2 — Worked example

Use the sleep / late-work example above or another neutral body-domain example.

Show the conversion:

```text
«у меня нет режима»
→
one bounded event
```

Keep the example short enough that the new operation remains obvious.

### Phase 3 — One explicit distinction

Teach only:

> **Описание себя — не то же самое, что описание произошедшего события.**

Why it matters:
- a global label is hard to test;
- one episode can be compared with reality;
- one episode preserves uncertainty;
- one episode can later be examined for where participation remained possible.

Boundary:

> A single episode does not prove a stable trait or repeating pattern.

### Phase 4 — Guided application

Teacher/AI holds structure; learner provides content.

Recommended question path, one question at a time:

1. «Что конкретно произошло?»
2. «Когда и где это было?»
3. «Какие фактические условия в тот момент важно учесть?»
4. «Что ты действительно сделал или не сделал?»
5. «Что произошло сразу после этого?»
6. «Что из сказанного факт, а что уже твоя оценка или объяснение?» — only if needed.

If the learner moves into identity labels, return to the event:

> «Это может быть важным выводом. А что именно произошло в том эпизоде?»

If the learner searches for a deep cause:

> «Причину пока не требуется устанавливать. Сначала зафиксируем сам эпизод так, чтобы он был различим».

### Phase 5 — Route and present-action bridge

Lesson 1 must preserve the approved direction → current position → route → next-step axis without turning route design into a new Lesson 1 skill.

Ask only:

> **«Из того, что стало фактически видно сейчас, есть ли один небольшой безопасный шаг, который уже имеет смысл сделать или подготовить?»**

Possible outputs:
- prepare water, food, equipment or sleep conditions;
- stop one unnecessary continuation already underway;
- put one needed item in place;
- send one support request;
- no action now.

“No action now” is valid. The aim is to prevent analysis from becoming an endpoint, not to force a behavioral correction.

### Phase 6 — One field assignment

One task only:

> **When one body-relevant episode naturally occurs, capture it as one bounded event instead of a global conclusion.**

Minimum 30–90 second capture:

```text
when / where:
what happened:
relevant factual condition:
what I actually did / did not do:
immediate observable result:
```

No explanation required.

No continuous self-monitoring.

No requirement to catch the episode before it happens.

Late capture after the event is valid.

### Phase 7 — Neutral close

Suggested close:

> «До следующего занятия задача не в том, чтобы исправлять себя и не в том, чтобы найти причину. Нужен один реальный эпизод, который удастся увидеть как отдельное событие. Если заметишь его только после — этого достаточно».

## 9. Compact learner artifact

Lesson 1 produces exactly one compact artifact:

```text
Created direction: [short]
Episode:
- when / where
- relevant conditions
- realized action / inaction
- immediate observable result
Current position after the episode: [one factual sentence]
One relevant resource/support, if visible: [optional]
```

The artifact is input for Lesson 2. It is not a personality profile and not a pattern claim.

## 10. Completion signal

Lesson 1 is complete enough to progress when the learner can take a statement such as:

> «Я такой / у меня всегда так / я никогда не могу…»

and return to:

> **one bounded event with observable details and uncertainty preserved.**

Completion does not require:
- behavior change;
- desired health outcome;
- early interruption of automaticity;
- a correct explanation;
- identifying a repeating scenario.

## 11. Common failure modes

### 11.1 Personality verdict replaces event description

Response: ask for time, place and realized action.

### 11.2 The learner produces a week/month summary

Response: choose one occurrence, not the aggregate.

### 11.3 The teacher explains the cause

Response: return to facts; hypotheses are not needed for Lesson 1.

### 11.4 The learner chooses the worst failure

Response: relevance to direction is the criterion, not shame intensity.

### 11.5 The lesson turns into behavior correction

Response: present-action bridge may produce one safe step, but full participation training belongs later.

### 11.6 One episode is generalized into a pattern

Response: explicitly preserve Stage 2 boundary. One episode is one episode.

### 11.7 Too much data are collected

Response: use only the five minimum episode fields. The daily questionnaire remains separate telemetry.

## 12. Epistemic boundaries

Maintain throughout:

```text
fact ≠ interpretation
intended ≠ realized
one episode ≠ stable pattern
unknown ≠ failure
late noticing ≠ useless noticing
```

Do not infer hidden motives, diagnoses or “true needs.”

## 13. Safety boundary

Lesson 1 is educational, not medical treatment.

Do not encourage episode capture while driving, operating equipment, working at height or during other dangerous/time-critical activity. Capture can occur afterward.

Acute medical or safety issues override the lesson flow.

## 14. Relation to Lesson 2

Lesson 1 creates a reliable unit of observation.

Lesson 2 adds a different operation:

> **reconstruct functionally connected links inside one episode without requiring a hidden root cause.**

Therefore Lesson 1 should stop once the episode is bounded and minimally factual.

Correct handoff:

```text
Lesson 1:
this is the episode

Lesson 2:
how did the distinguishable links within this episode connect?
```

## 15. Quality gate before writing the script

The Lesson 1 script should not be written until this brief is accepted or revised by the Owner.

Review questions:

1. Is the lesson narrow enough to avoid teaching Lesson 2 early?
2. Is “global label ≠ bounded episode” the only central distinction?
3. Is the worked example appropriate and non-moralizing?
4. Are the five minimum episode fields sufficient?
5. Is the present-action bridge light enough not to become Lesson 4?
6. Is the field task simple enough for 30–90 second capture?
7. Is the direction / current-position axis visible without overloading the learner?
8. Are successful and unsuccessful episodes both valid?
9. Is questionnaire telemetry kept separate?
10. Is the Stage 2 pattern boundary preserved?
