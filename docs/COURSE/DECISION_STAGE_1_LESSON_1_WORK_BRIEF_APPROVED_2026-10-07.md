## Decision

## Binding lesson boundary

Lesson 1 teaches one new operation only:

```text
global conclusion / vague self-description
→ one bounded factual episode
```

The lesson must stop before full causal reconstruction, hidden-cause search, repeating-pattern analysis, alternative-regulation teaching or the complete Selection Point cycle.

A successful episode and an unsuccessful episode are equally valid if they are relevant to the participant-owned body direction.

The five minimum episode fields remain:

1. what happened;
2. when / where;
3. relevant factual conditions;
4. realized action / inaction;
5. immediate observable result.

Unknown remains valid. Late noticing remains valid. Questionnaire telemetry remains separate.
