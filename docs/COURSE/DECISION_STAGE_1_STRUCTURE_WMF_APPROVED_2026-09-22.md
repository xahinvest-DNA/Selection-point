**Date:** 22 September 2026  
**Status:** approved  
**Authority:** Owner explicit approval — «Утверждаем»

## Decision

Approve the Williams / Millman / Fritz research synthesis and apply it to the Stage 1 “Effective Body” course structure.

Approved course structure:

~~~text
ORIENTATION
created direction + current reality

→ LESSON 1
one concrete relevant episode

→ LESSON 2
local episode structure

→ LESSON 3
state ≠ familiar regulation means

→ LESSON 4
one available move serving the created direction

→ LESSON 5
realization → consequence → updated current reality → next move

→ INTEGRATION
several separate comparable episodes
~~~

## Binding interpretation

- Five core lessons remain.
- Orientation and integration are not counted as lessons.
- The course is not organized around problem elimination alone.
- Participant-owned created direction and factual current reality form the course-wide axis.
- Millman's present-action discipline is cross-cutting, not a separate mindfulness lesson.
- Williams's level-appropriate tools principle is cross-cutting; Stage 2 scenario analysis stays outside Stage 1.
- Fritz's structural tension is used as a design map, not as a proven deterministic psychological law.
- The daily trajectory questionnaire remains a separate telemetry stream.
- Stage 1 must not require discovery of a recurring scenario.
- Full lesson writing may now proceed from the approved structure.

Design the reusable learner-facing lesson template / delivery mechanics before writing all five lesson scripts.
