## Decision

Refine the Stage 1 course entry without changing the approved five-core-lesson sequence.

### Orientation

Orientation establishes the learner's coordinate system:

```text
participant-owned created result / direction
+
factual current reality relative to that result
```

Orientation does **not** require selecting or analysing an episode.

Its role is to prevent the course from beginning as problem removal and to establish the Fritz-style creative orientation before episode work begins.

### Lesson 1

Lesson 1 is the first trainable Stage 1 operation.

Its purpose is:

> **Make one relevant part of current reality observable through one concrete episode.**

Working chain:

```text
created result / direction
+
current reality
→ choose one relevant part of that reality
→ locate one concrete episode in which it became observable
→ preserve the episode as factual material for Lesson 2
```

The episode is not the organizing goal of the course and is not selected because it is the learner's worst failure. It is a level-appropriate observation tool for making current reality concrete.

## Williams / Millman / Fritz interpretation

- **Fritz:** created result + current reality organize the course; episode work serves this axis rather than replacing it.
- **Millman:** current reality must remain grounded in ordinary lived events rather than abstract self-description; later action returns to ordinary life.
- **Williams:** Stage 1 uses one level-appropriate observational tool and does not prematurely introduce global underlying-structure or Stage 2 pattern analysis.

## Preserved boundaries

- No required teacher, facilitator or AI assistant.
