## Binding interpretation

- Orientation sets the coordinate system and does not require episode selection.
- Lesson 1 has one main operation: grounding one relevant part of current reality in one bounded concrete episode.
- The episode is a means of observing current reality, not the organizing goal of the course.
- Lesson 1 does not require behavior change.
- Lesson 1 ends before causal reconstruction or functional-chain analysis.
- The resulting episode becomes direct input for Lesson 2.
- The five-core-lesson sequence and self-paced delivery format remain unchanged.
- Fritz provides the `created result + current reality` axis.
- Millman provides the return from abstraction to ordinary lived reality.
- Williams provides developmental discipline: one level-appropriate observational tool without premature global-structure analysis.
