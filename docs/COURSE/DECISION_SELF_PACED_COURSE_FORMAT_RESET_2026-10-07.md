# Decision — Self-Paced Course Format Reset

**ID:** SP-COURSE-DEC-SELF-PACED-001  
**Date:** 7 October 2026  
**Status:** approved  
**Authority:** Owner correction in project chat

## Decision

The Stage 1 course is a **self-paced online course**.

Its core delivery does not depend on a teacher, facilitator or AI assistant.

Each lesson must be designed around:

```text
theoretical material
+
independent practical work by the learner
+
field application in ordinary life
+
learner-visible result / artifact
```

Optional future software or support may assist the learner, but it may not be required for the core lesson to function.

## Correction

The previously approved `STAGE_1_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` v2 incorrectly encoded teacher/AI-led delivery. It is reopened for revision.

`STAGE_1_LESSON_1_WORK_BRIEF.md` is also reopened because its delivery mapping inherits that assumption.

`STAGE_1_LESSON_1.md` is withdrawn from active development because it was built as a teacher/AI-led script.

The approved five-lesson Stage 1 course structure remains in force. This correction changes delivery mechanics, not the approved course sequence or Foundation meaning.
