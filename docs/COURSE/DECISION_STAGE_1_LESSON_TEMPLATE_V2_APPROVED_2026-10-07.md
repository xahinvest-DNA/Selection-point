## Decision

Approve `docs/COURSE/STAGE_1_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` version 2 as the delivery template for the five Stage 1 / Effective Body lessons.

## Binding additions in v2

- direction → current position → route → next step → realized action → feedback;
- current position includes relevant load, limits and available resources;
- attention is treated as a project resource and is redirected toward effective movement, capabilities and support;
- no single weakness, including alcohol, becomes the default center of the course;
- route quality is assessed through consequences and the next factual position;
- questionnaire exposure remains visible as a possible scaffold or intervention;
- the course does not require an ideal route, a universal opposite action or a sixth lesson.

The attention-routing principle is an owner-origin design proposition inspired by the user's reading of Sun Tzu. It is not recorded as a historical quotation or as an independently verified claim about Sun Tzu's wording.

## Authorization boundary

The template is approved. Preparation of the Lesson 1 work brief may become the next project action. This decision does not itself create the Lesson 1 script, open a new Foundation stage, or convert questionnaire telemetry into proof of efficacy.
